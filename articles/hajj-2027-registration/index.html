Hajj 2027 Registration - Dates, Stages and What to Prepare

Hajj 2027, the 1448H pilgrimage, is expected around mid-May 2027, with the Day of Arafah most likely on 15 May, subject to moon sighting. If you are planning to go, the work starts now, not in spring. Registration on Nusuk Hajj is open, and every profile was reset for the new season, so returning pilgrims must submit their documents again. Packages are not live yet, and no 2027 prices have been announced.

What Nusuk Hajj is, and who it is for: Nusuk Hajj is the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah platform for pilgrims from Muslim-minority countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand. Pilgrims from large Muslim-majority countries, such as Pakistan, Malaysia, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia and Morocco, go through their own national quotas and schemes instead. Find out which route applies to you before you start, because the two doors have different dates and different forms.

The expected timeline: The dates below come from the preliminary timeline reported in November 2026. None of them is posted on hajj.nusuk.sa yet, and the Ministry can change them at any time. Packages are expected to be set up on about 10 December 2026. Package viewing, customisation and e-wallet linking are expected from about 24 December 2026. Booking is expected to open on about 9 January 2027. Registration, meaning account creation and document submission, is already open. Do not trust any other date you see quoted as final until it appears on hajj.nusuk.sa itself.

The ten stages inside Nusuk Hajj: Once you register, your application moves through ten tracked stages: create an account, upload your documents, fill in the application, application verification, select your preferred category, activate your e-wallet, browse service providers, select your package, book your package, and pay. Verification now takes hours to days. The e-wallet is locked for the moment, so there is nothing to pay yet, and you should never pay anyone outside your own Nusuk account.

The two files that block most people: Nusuk accepts only a passport scan and a photo in a strict size and format, and applications with the wrong files can be rejected or delayed. Prepare both files exactly as the platform asks before you start. Two more rules to know: Nusuk only accepts applicants who are 16 or older when they register, and if you register as a resident, your residence card must be valid through December 2027. If you registered in an earlier season, log in with that same account; do not create a new one.

What to do now: Create your Nusuk Hajj account and submit your documents now, while there is no rush. Subscribe to the platform updates so you hear the day packages go live. If your country runs a national scheme, check its dates separately: Pakistan's government-scheme applications for 2027 opened on 18 August 2026, for example. Do not pay anyone who promises a guaranteed 2027 package before packages go live, and do not believe any 2027 price quoted today: no prices have been published, and anyone quoting them is guessing.

This page will be updated the day the Ministry announces the opening dates. Register now, prepare your documents, and wait for the official announcement.
