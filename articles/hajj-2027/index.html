Hajj 2027 — Dates, Registration & Complete Guide

Hajj is the fifth pillar of Islam and the journey of a lifetime — and for Hajj 2027 (1448 AH), the planning starts now. This guide brings together the expected dates, how registration through Nusuk Hajj works, and a short summary of the rites, so you can prepare early and calmly.

Please note — the dates are estimates: Every Gregorian date in this guide comes from the Umm al-Qura calendar and is an estimate only. The days of Hajj are confirmed only by the official sighting of the Dhul Hijjah moon, announced by Saudi Arabia's Supreme Court. If the moon is sighted a day earlier or later, every date below moves with it.

The expected dates for Hajj 2027:

  • Hajj days: Friday 14 May to Wednesday 19 May 2027 — corresponding to 8–13 Dhul Hijjah 1448 AH
  • Day of Arafah (9 Dhul Hijjah): Saturday 15 May 2027 — the greatest day of Hajj
  • Eid al-Adha (10 Dhul Hijjah): Sunday 16 May 2027
The call to Hajj: Allah says in the Qur'an: "And proclaim to the people the Hajj; they will come to you on foot and on every lean camel; they will come from every distant pass" (Qur'an 22:27). And the Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever performs Hajj for the sake of Allah and does not commit any obscenity or wrongdoing, returns as pure as the day his mother gave birth to him" — narrated in Sahih al-Bukhari (1521) and Sahih Muslim (1350).

Registration — only through Nusuk Hajj: For pilgrims from countries served by the programme, Hajj registration and packages are handled only through the official Nusuk Hajj platform — hajj.nusuk.sa. As of checks in late September 2026, the 1448 AH season had not yet opened. Registration is expected to open in autumn 2026 (October–December), with package sales following in early 2027 — but no opening date has been officially announced, so check hajj.nusuk.sa directly and beware of anyone quoting you a "confirmed" date or price. Pilgrims from countries not served by Nusuk Hajj (such as Pakistan and India) continue to apply through their own national Hajj schemes and licensed operators.

How registration works — step by step:

  • Step 1 — Create and verify your Nusuk profile: Register on hajj.nusuk.sa with your personal details exactly as they appear on your passport, and complete the verification steps. (If you are new to Nusuk, our Nusuk App Guide explains how the platform and app work.)
  • Step 2 — Passport: You need a passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date — renew early if yours expires before the end of 2027.
  • Step 3 — Photo: Upload a recent passport-style photograph that meets the portal's specification.
  • Step 4 — Health and vaccination requirements: Complete the health declaration and the vaccination requirements as announced for the season — check the portal for the exact list when registration opens.
  • Step 5 — Choose a package: When sales open, compare the official packages (category, hotels in Makkah and Madinah, Mina camp, transport) and select the one that fits your budget and mobility.
  • Step 6 — Pay through the portal: Payment is made inside Nusuk Hajj — never to an agent's personal account.
  • Step 7 — Hajj visa: Once your package is confirmed and paid, your Hajj visa is issued electronically through Nusuk.

The rites of Hajj — a short summary:

  • Before the days begin: Enter Ihram at the Miqat with the intention for Hajj and the Talbiyah on your lips.
  • 8 Dhul Hijjah — Mina: Travel to Mina and spend the day and night there in prayer.
  • 9 Dhul Hijjah — Arafah: The heart of Hajj. Stand at Arafah from noon until sunset in du'a and repentance — "Hajj is Arafah." After sunset, move on without praying Maghrib there.
  • The night — Muzdalifah: Spend the night under the open sky at Muzdalifah, pray Maghrib and Isha together, rest, and collect your pebbles.
  • 10 Dhul Hijjah — Eid day: Stone the large Jamarah (Jamrat al-Aqabah), offer the sacrifice, shave or trim the hair, then perform Tawaf al-Ifadah (and Sa'i) in Makkah.
  • 11–13 Dhul Hijjah — Days of Tashreeq: Stay in Mina and stone all three Jamarat each day after noon.
  • Before leaving Makkah: Perform the Farewell Tawaf (Tawaf al-Wada) as your last rite.

Quick questions:

  • When is Hajj 2027? Hajj 2027 is expected from 14 to 19 May 2027 (8–13 Dhul Hijjah 1448 AH), with the Day of Arafah on Saturday 15 May and Eid al-Adha on Sunday 16 May 2027. These are Umm al-Qura calendar estimates — the final dates depend on the official Dhul Hijjah moon sighting.
  • How do I register for Hajj 2027? Only through the official Nusuk Hajj platform at hajj.nusuk.sa: create and verify your profile, add a passport valid at least 6 months beyond your return and a passport-style photo, complete the health and vaccination requirements as announced, then choose a package and pay through the portal — your Hajj visa is issued via Nusuk. The 1448 season had not opened as of late September 2026 and is expected to open in autumn 2026; check the portal directly for the announcement.
  • How much does Hajj 2027 cost? Nobody knows yet — no 2027 pricing is confirmed. Prices appear only inside Nusuk Hajj when package sales open in early 2027. Treat any price quoted before then, especially by unofficial agents, as a warning sign.
