How to Get a Rawdah Permit on the Nusuk App: Step by Step (2026)

The Rawdah, also called Al-Rawdah Al-Sharifah, is a very blessed place inside Masjid an-Nabawi in Madinah. It is the area between the house of the Prophet ﷺ and his minbar (pulpit).

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Between my house and my minbar is one of the gardens of Paradise" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196; Sahih Muslim 1391). This is why Muslims from all over the world wish to pray there.

A permit is mandatory in 2026. You cannot walk in without a Nusuk permit — walk-in entry without a permit is not allowed. The good news is that the permit is completely free. Do not pay anyone for it.

Book before you travel to Madinah, not after you arrive. Slots are limited and they fill very fast. If you wait until you reach Madinah, you may find no slots left for the days of your stay.

How to book — step by step:

  • Step 1 — Download the official Nusuk app. Get it from the App Store or Google Play. Check that the publisher is the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — beware of imitation apps with a similar name or logo.
  • Step 2 — Register and complete your profile. Sign in or register with your passport number and your Umrah or tourist visa number. Complete your profile first, before you try to book.
  • Step 3 — Open Prophet's Mosque Services. On the home screen, go to Prophet's Mosque Services (shown in some versions as Holy Mosque Services).
  • Step 4 — Choose the Rawdah service. Tap "Praying in the Noble Rawdah" (also shown as "Book Rawdah Visit"). Men and women have separate services and separate time slots — choose the correct gender category carefully. A permit booked under the wrong category will not scan at the gate.
  • Step 5 — Select the visitors. Tick your own name. If you are booking for family members or companions, add them too — they must be linked to your account.
  • Step 6 — Pick a date and time. Choose a green available date and time slot. Grey or full slots cannot be booked.
  • Step 7 — Confirm and issue the permit. Confirm your booking, accept the terms, and tap Issue Permit. Your permit with a QR code appears instantly. Take a screenshot and save it — mobile signal in the mosque courtyard can be weak, and you will need to show the QR code at the gate.
  • Step 8 — Arrive early on the day. Go to the gate shown on your permit and arrive 20–30 minutes early. Staff will scan your QR code before you enter.

Important things to know:

  • Slots are normally released 7–14 days in advance. In peak seasons like Ramadan, they may open only 3–5 days ahead.
  • Slots fill within minutes. Start checking the app daily from 10 days before your visit and refresh often.
  • The full visit lasts about 30–45 minutes and the time is strictly enforced. Your time inside the Rawdah itself is short — around 10 minutes — so prepare your duas in advance.
  • The general rule is one Rawdah permit per person every 365 days — in simple words, one visit per year per person.
  • The app also offers Instant Track: when you are near the mosque, you can request an immediate slot every 20 minutes, subject to capacity.
  • The app also has a Waitlist — join it and you will be notified when a slot frees up.
  • Since 31 October 2026, the permit can also be issued on the Nusuk web portal at services.nusuk.sa — look for "Issue a Rawdah prayer permit".

What to do inside the Rawdah:

Once you are inside, stay calm and do not push anyone. Pray 2 rak'at, make sincere dua for yourself, your family and the whole Ummah, and send salawat (peace and blessings) upon the Prophet ﷺ. Keep your worship brief and focused so that other pilgrims waiting outside also get their turn.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Is the Rawdah permit free? Yes. The Rawdah permit is completely free in the official Nusuk app.
  • Can I visit the Rawdah without a permit? No. In 2026 a Nusuk permit is required — walk-in entry without a permit is not allowed.
  • How early should I book? As soon as slots open — normally 7–14 days ahead. Check the app daily starting 10 days before your visit.
