Ihram Explained: What Becomes Forbidden, and Why

Ihram is not just clothing — it is a sacred state entered with intention. Allah commands regarding Hajj: "…let there be no obscenity, nor wickedness, nor wrangling in the Hajj" (Qur'an 2:197). Whoever performs Hajj avoiding these returns "as pure as the day his mother bore him" (Bukhari & Muslim).

  • Clothing (men): no sewn garments — no shirt, trousers, turban or hooded cloak, and no cloth touched by saffron or wars perfume; if sandals are unavailable, leather socks may be worn cut below the ankles (hadith of Ibn Umar, Bukhari & Muslim)
  • Women: normal clothing is worn, but no face veil (niqab) tied over the face and no gloves in Ihram
  • Perfume: none on body or clothes after entering Ihram — for men and women
  • Also forbidden: cutting hair or nails, hunting land game, marital relations and whatever leads to them, arguing and sinful speech
  • Forgotten mistakes: scholars note that what is done forgetfully or in ignorance is treated more leniently — ask a scholar about your specific case rather than worrying alone
