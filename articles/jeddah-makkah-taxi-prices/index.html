Jeddah Airport to Makkah — Taxi, Train & Bus Prices (2026)

Jeddah's King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED) is the main gateway for Makkah: from the airport to Masjid al-Haram is about 85–95 km by road, roughly 60–90 minutes without traffic. You have five real options — train, ride-hailing, airport taxi, bus, or a pre-booked transfer. The prices below are researched ranges checked in November 2026; fares vary by date, demand and season, so always confirm the live price before you pay.

Good to know, option by option:

  • Train: the Haramain station is inside Terminal 1; book on sar.hhr.sa or the HHR app. Makkah station is in Rusaifah, about 3–4 km from the Haram, so add a taxi (SAR 20–40, 10–15 minutes) or the SAR 4 city bus for the last leg.
  • Uber / Careem: the simplest door-to-door choice — best for families, lots of luggage and late-night arrivals.
  • Airport taxi: agree the price before you get in; quotes of SAR 300–500 at the rank are common, above the usual going rate.
  • Bus: the cheapest option, but it drops you at the bus station, not your hotel, so allow for a short taxi at the Makkah end as well.
  • Private transfer: a fixed price agreed in advance, with meet and greet at arrivals — the calmest option after a long flight.

Which should you choose? The train is best for solo travellers and couples with light luggage landing at Terminal 1 in the daytime. For a family, Uber or Careem is usually simpler and often cheaper overall once the station taxi is added to the train fare. All fares rise as travel dates approach, and again in Ramadan and the Hajj season. And if you land in ihram, keep the free pilgrim shuttle in mind where it runs. Continuing on to Madinah afterwards?
