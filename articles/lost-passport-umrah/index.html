Lost Passport During Umrah: What to Do Step by Step

Short answer: Losing your passport in Makkah or Madinah is serious but fixable, as long as you act in order and not all at once. Retrace your steps and check the Haram lost-and-found, file a police report, get an Emergency Travel Document from your embassy or consulate, get the Jawazat exit stamp, and then fly home. Each office asks for the paper from the step before, so skipping a step only sends you back.

Step 1, retrace and check lost property: Stay calm and retrace your steps. Check the Haram lost-property desk, because items left in the mosque often reach it within hours. Ask at your hotel front desk too, because the hotel holds a copy of your passport from check-in, and that copy speeds up everything that follows.

Step 2, file a police report: Report the loss at the nearest police station (Markaz al-Shurta). Your hotel can tell you which station covers your area and may send someone with you. Ask for the written report (Balagh Faqdan) and keep the stamped original safe, because every later office, the embassy, Jawazat and the airline, will ask for it.

Step 3, your embassy and the Emergency Travel Document: Tell your Umrah company or group leader the same day; the company issues a stamped printout of your details, which supports your case. Then go to your embassy or consulate. For Pakistani pilgrims, the Consulate General of Pakistan in Jeddah issues the Emergency Travel Document (ETD section +966 12 6692371, extensions 209 and 309, open Sunday to Thursday), and the Embassy in Riyadh can be reached on +966 11 4887272. Bring the police report, a copy of the lost passport, a copy of the Saudi visa, a CNIC copy, two passport photos, and your return ticket details. The ETD is a one-way document for the journey home only; it is not a replacement passport, so apply for a new passport once you are back home.

Step 4, the exit stamp and your flight: Visit the local Passport Department (Jawazat) to get the travel document stamped for exit; without that stamp you cannot board. Tell your airline the same day, so it can note your booking, accept the travel document, and protect your seat while the paperwork is completed. Keep every stamped paper together in one folder until you are home, because the airline check-in desk may ask to see the police report and the exit stamp together with the travel document.

Prevention, before you ever need this: Before departure, photograph your passport data page, your visa and your CNIC, and keep copies in your phone, in your email, and as a printed copy in a separate bag. In Saudi Arabia, keep the passport itself in the hotel safe and carry a copy while you are out. Most lost-passport cases become a one-day problem when copies are ready, and a much longer problem when they are not. Do this even if you are travelling in a group and the company holds your documents for part of the trip, because the copy in your own phone is the one you can reach at any hour, including the middle of the night.
