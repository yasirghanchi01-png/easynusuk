Madinah Airport to Masjid Nabawi: Taxi, Bus and Prices (2026)

Prince Mohammad bin Abdulaziz International Airport (MED) is the arrival point for most pilgrims visiting Madinah. The airport sits about 15 to 20 km from Masjid Nabawi, and the drive to the Central Haram area takes about 20 to 30 minutes in normal traffic. You have four real options: the official airport taxi, a pre-booked private transfer, the city bus, or a ride-hailing app. This guide gives the current prices and the honest pros and cons of each, plus what to do if you are heading straight on to Makkah.

Official airport taxi, fixed fare: The airport publishes a fixed tariff for its official taxis: SAR 75 for a car carrying up to five passengers, and SAR 90 for a larger vehicle carrying up to seven, to the Central Haram area, a trip of about 20 km. Use the official taxi queue inside the terminal and the licensed operators who run it, including Imtiaz Taibah, SATCO, Lemo Safwa and Al Safi Taxi. Because the fare is fixed by the tariff, there is nothing to negotiate and nothing extra to agree; if a driver asks for more than the tariff, simply walk to the next car in the queue.

Private pre-booked transfers: Hotels and transfer companies sell pre-booked pickups, with sedans starting from about SAR 150 per vehicle. That is roughly double the official taxi fare for the same road, so it is worth it only if you want a driver waiting with a name board, help with your luggage, and a car arranged in advance for a large family or a very late flight. For most pilgrims, the official taxi makes the same journey for less money and with no booking at all.

Madinah Bus Route 400, the budget option: Route 400 of the Madinah Bus network runs between the airport and the Prophet's Mosque 24 hours a day, with buses about every 30 minutes. The ride takes 25 to 40 minutes depending on traffic and stops. The fare is only a few riyals; published reports vary between SAR 3.45 and SAR 14, so confirm the current fare at the stop before you ride. The bus is the best option for a solo traveller with light bags, and the least practical for a family arriving tired with heavy suitcases.

Uber, Careem and the train question: Uber and Careem both operate at Madinah airport and are a comfortable middle option, with the fare shown in the app before you ride. One common confusion needs clearing up: there is NO Haramain high-speed train station at Madinah airport. The Madinah Haramain station is a city terminal, and a taxi from the airport to that station costs from about SAR 100, so the train only makes sense if you are genuinely travelling on to Makkah or Jeddah, not to reach the Haram.

Heading straight to Makkah: If your plan is to go directly to Makkah, take a taxi from the airport to the SAPTCO station in Madinah, about SAR 15 to 25 and 10 to 15 minutes away. From there, the SAPTCO bus to Makkah costs about SAR 80 to 85 per person and takes 5 to 6 hours. The bus stops for 15 to 20 minutes at the miqat of Masjid Dhul Hulayfah (Abyar Ali), just outside Madinah, where pilgrims enter ihram, so have your ihram ready and do not sleep through the stop.

Practical tips: If you land late at night, use the official taxi queue; it is staffed around the clock and the fixed fare removes any argument. Keep some small cash in Saudi riyals for your first hours, even if you plan to pay by app later. Have your hotel name and its location near the Haram ready to show the driver, ideally in Arabic from your booking confirmation. And if your group travels on to Makkah later in the trip, shared vans leaving from near the Prophet's Mosque charge about SAR 50 per person, a useful middle option between the bus and a private car.
