Hotels Near Bab al-Salam Gate, Madinah: Best Stays by the Prophet's Mosque

Bab al-Salam, the Gate of Peace, is the historic western gate of Masjid an-Nabawi, the gate pilgrims use to greet the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him. Staying on this western side puts you closest to the Rawdah entrance and to the ladies entrance, which makes it the best base for women, elderly pilgrims and anyone planning several visits to the Rawdah. If your Madinah days are short, those saved minutes at every prayer add up to more time inside the mosque and less time walking across hot courtyards.

Closest hotels to Bab al-Salam: The hotels below sit on the western side of the mosque, listed with walking distances from UmrahMap.

Walking times vary with the entrance route used and with crowds at prayer times, so treat every figure here as a guide rather than a promise.

Five star stays on the western piazza: Pullman Zamzam Madinah is a 5 star hotel on the Bab al-Salam side, with rooms listed from around USD 149 per night, about 835 rooms, and a partial renovation completed in 2024. Madinah Hilton is a 5 star hotel on the western piazza with a view towards the Green Dome, rooms listed from around USD 299 per night, about 357 rooms, and a Rawdah permit assistance desk for guests. Anwar Al Madinah Movenpick Hotel is a 5 star stay about a 3 minute walk from the mosque, with about 1,295 rooms, close to the ladies entrance. Listed prices are from August 2026, and readers should check current rates before booking.

Budget picks near the gate: Manazeli Madinah and Al-Madinah Golden are the closest budget options, then Rua Al-Andalus and Nusk Al-Hijra, all a short flat walk from the gate. These simpler hotels are good for families and for pilgrims who spend most of the day at the mosque and use the room mainly for sleep, rest and a shower between prayers. What you give up in lobby size and breakfast choice, you gain back in location.

Why the west side suits women and elderly pilgrims: The ladies entrance, Gate 25, is on this side, a short walk from the hotels above. The walk is flat, shaded and level, with no steep streets to manage five times a day. The Rawdah entrance is near too, so a permit slot means a short walk instead of a long one across the courtyards. Book ahead, and if no scheduled slot appears, our Rawdah Instant Access guide explains the other route in.

Booking tips: Book early, because prices rise sharply in Ramadan and Hajj season. Compare the full stay total including meal plan before paying, not just the nightly rate. Confirm the actual entrance route from the hotel, not just the straight line distance on a map. Allow extra time at Fajr, Maghrib and Friday prayers, when the courtyards and gates fill quickly. Gates can be closed or made one way at prayer times, so follow mosque staff directions. For the wider choice across the city, see our Madinah hotels guide, plan your Madinah ziyarat days between prayers, and leave time for Jannatul Baqi, the historic cemetery east of the mosque.
