Masjid Quba: Two Rakat with the Reward of an Umrah

Masjid Quba, on the southern edge of Madinah, is the first mosque built in Islam — founded when the Prophet ﷺ arrived during the Hijrah. Allah praises it in the Qur'an as "a mosque founded on righteousness from the first day" (Qur'an 9:108).

The promise: "Whoever purifies himself in his house, then comes to the Quba Mosque and offers one prayer in it, will have a reward like that of an Umrah" (Sunan Ibn Majah 1412; also Jami' at-Tirmidhi 324). Ibn Umar (RA) reported that the Prophet ﷺ used to go to Quba every Saturday, walking or riding, and pray two rakat there (Sahih al-Bukhari 1193–1194; Sahih Muslim 1399).
  • Do wudu where you are staying, before you set out — the wudu at home is part of the narration's wording
  • Pray two rakat (or any prayer) inside Masjid Quba
  • Many pilgrims walk the pleasant Quba pedestrian route from Masjid an-Nabawi (about 3.5 km)
