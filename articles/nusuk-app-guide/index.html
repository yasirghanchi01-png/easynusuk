Nusuk App Complete Guide — Download, Setup, Visa, Rawdah Permit & More

Almost everything about Umrah now runs through one app. Nusuk is the official app and web platform of Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah — the single account through which pilgrims apply for visas, book permits and plan the whole journey. It replaces the old Eatmarna platform and brings together more than 130 digital services under one account. By April 2026 it had passed 51 million users from more than 190 countries (Arab News, reported by Wego). This guide covers the app in full: downloading it safely, setting up your account, what it can do, and the 2026 rules that catch pilgrims out.

Visas and permits are issued only inside the official Nusuk app and through the official Nusuk websites linked at the end of this guide.

Download it — and only from the official stores: Nusuk is free on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Search for "Nusuk" (نسك) and check that the publisher is the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah before you install. Never install APK files from third-party websites — fake clones of the app exist and are built to steal passport and visa data. You can also use Nusuk in a browser at nusuk.sa. Hajj registration runs on its own separate platform at hajj.nusuk.sa — our Hajj 2027 guide explains how that side works.

Setting up your account — step by step:

  • Step 1 — Choose your language. The app works in about 10 languages, including Arabic, English, Urdu, Turkish, Malay, Indonesian, French and Hausa.
  • Step 2 — Tap Create Account.
  • Step 3 — Choose your account type: Visitor if you are an international pilgrim, or Resident if you hold a Saudi Iqama.
  • Step 4 — Enter your details: your passport number (or Iqama number for residents), your name, email address and a secure password — exactly as they appear on your documents.
  • Step 5 — Verify the OTP sent to your phone or email.

The safest moment to create the account is as soon as your visa is issued — register then, not on the day you arrive, so permit booking is already open to you. Travelling for the first time? Work through our Umrah for First-Timers guide alongside this one.

What you can do inside the app:

  • Apply for an Umrah eVisa directly, if your nationality is eligible.
  • Book your Umrah permit. The permit carries a QR code and is mandatory in 2026: officials at the gates of Masjid al-Haram scan the QR, and a valid visa alone is no longer enough to enter.
  • Book a Rawdah permit in Madinah — a separate booking from the Umrah permit. Our Rawdah Permit guide walks through it step by step.
  • Book hotels and transport for the journey.
  • Use Nusuk Wallet for payments inside Saudi Arabia.
  • Follow prayer times, maps and guidance inside the holy sites.

2026 rules to know before you travel:

  • "Any Visa Is an Umrah Visa": holders of a Saudi tourist, transit or personal visit visa can perform Umrah, as long as the visa is linked to Nusuk before arrival and a permit is issued.
  • Multiple-entry Umrah visa: launched on 20 July 2026 and valid for 365 days from issuance.
  • Enter within 30 days: once an Umrah visa is issued, you must enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the issue date — plan your dates before you apply.
  • Accommodation first: since June 2026, pilgrims applying for an Umrah visa through Nusuk must first book their accommodation through the official Nusuk Masar platform.
  • One Rawdah permit a year: the advance Rawdah permit is limited to one per person every 365 days, so choose your slot carefully.
  • Zero-data access: the app includes a zero-data access feature, so pilgrims can use its essential services without consuming mobile data.

Visa types and what they cost are set out in our Umrah Visa Fees guide.

Practical tips that save the day:

  • Screenshots do not work at the gates. Your entry QR code is generated live on the server and refreshes — an old screenshot will fail the scan. You need live internet as you approach the gate, so keep a Saudi eSIM set as your data line at every checkpoint.
  • Book Rawdah slots as early as your dates allow. In peak season they fill within minutes of release.
  • Start checking the app the moment your travel dates are confirmed — permits and slots reward pilgrims who book early.

Quick questions:

  • Is the Nusuk app free? Yes — the app is free to download and use, and the Umrah and Rawdah permits themselves are free for pilgrims holding a valid visa.
  • Do I need Nusuk if I already have a visa? Yes. In 2026 a visa on its own does not open the gates of Masjid al-Haram: the visa must be linked to Nusuk and a permit with a QR code issued before you enter.
  • Can I book the Rawdah without Nusuk? No — for international visitors the Rawdah permit is booked only through Nusuk, and it is a separate booking from your Umrah permit. One advance permit is allowed per person every 365 days.
