Nusuk OTP Not Received? How to Fix It (2026)

The OTP is the four-digit verification code Nusuk sends when you sign up or log in. When it never arrives, you cannot get into the app at all. Most OTP failures have one of four causes: a wrong number format, a visa that is too new to be synced, the wrong delivery channel, or an old account blocking the way. Work through the fixes below in order.

Check your number first: Enter your number with the full country code and no leading zero after it. A UK number starting 07 must be entered as +44 without the 0, for example 07912 345678 becomes +447912345678. Remove all spaces, dashes and brackets. Check that international SMS is switched on with your mobile company, and that the number matches the one on your visa record. Nusuk checks your details against immigration records, so a number that does not match the visa process can fail verification. One mistyped digit fails silently, so retype the number slowly and check every digit.

Wait out the visa sync delay: If your visa was issued less than 24 to 48 hours ago, Nusuk may not have your record yet. "ID not found" and "Something went wrong" during this window are often a sync delay, not a broken account. Wait a full day after the visa is issued, then try again. Do not keep creating new accounts while you wait. Each new attempt tangles the next one, and the old record is the one Nusuk remembers.

Try the email code and the web version: Newer versions of the Nusuk app offer the code by email as well as SMS. If SMS fails, choose the email option and check your spam and promotions folders, not just your inbox. Wait a minute before tapping resend. Tapping resend again and again can make the delay worse. If the app keeps failing, try the web version at nusuk.sa. Pilgrims report that one channel sometimes works when the other does not. If nothing works after repeated tries across a day, it is a server-side issue only Nusuk can fix, so contact support.

The old-account login loop: If you performed Umrah before, you may already have a Nusuk account from that trip, and a new sign-up will fail with "ID already exists" or loop back to the start. Use Recover Account, not New Account, and feed it the exact details from the web version. The recovery flow reattaches the old account instead of fighting it. If recovery still loops, email support@umrah.nusuk.sa with the subject RESET ID NUSUK and attach screenshots of your passport and visa. Do not keep creating new accounts. One identity, one account, one recovery.

When to call support: If none of the fixes above work, call Nusuk technical support on 920002814. Inside Saudi Arabia you can also call the pilgrim line 1966, or the unified contact number 930. Before you call, take a screenshot of the exact error message and note the time it happened. Never share your password or any verification code with anyone, including someone who says they are from support. Real support will never ask for them.

In most cases the fix is a number format or a one-day wait. Screenshot the error, try the fixes in order, and contact support only if the code still does not arrive after a full day.
