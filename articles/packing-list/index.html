Umrah Packing List — What to Pack for Umrah (Complete Checklist)

Packing for Umrah is not like packing for a holiday. You will enter Ihram on the way, walk several kilometres a day between the Haram and your hotel, and spend long hours in worship — so your bag should carry what serves the journey and nothing that burdens it. This is the complete 2026 checklist for men and women: work through it group by group and you will arrive ready.

From the Qur'an: Allah says: "And take provisions, but indeed, the best provision is fear of Allah (taqwa)" (Qur'an 2:197). Pack your bag with care — then remember that the provision which matters most on this journey is a heart full of taqwa.

1. Documents — always in your cabin bag:

  • Passport, valid for at least 6 months from your travel date
  • Visa printout — a printed copy of your visa, with the PDF saved on your phone as a backup. Check the cost and type before you apply with our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide
  • Flight and hotel booking confirmations for Makkah and Madinah
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Prescription medicines in their original packaging with a doctor's note — see the Health list below
  • Nusuk app installed and your account created before you fly, with permits booked or screenshotted — you will need it from day one
  • Vaccination proof where required — such as the meningitis ACWY certificate; check the current requirement for your country before you travel
  • Photocopies of everything, kept separately from the originals, plus copies saved on your phone

2. Ihram clothing:

  • Men: two unstitched white cloths — one to wear and one clean spare — plus a belt or pouch for your phone, cash and hotel key, because Ihram has no pockets. While in Ihram, men wear no stitched clothes. Pack one full set in your cabin bag: your flight may cross the Miqat before landing
  • Women: a loose abaya, hijab and a prayer dress — enough for the whole stay, in fabrics that wash and dry quickly — and comfortable modest clothing underneath
  • Unscented toiletries: while in Ihram, use no scented products — pack unscented soap, shampoo and deodorant, unscented wipes and an unscented lip balm, plus a toothbrush and miswak. Your normal scented items can be used before you enter Ihram and after you leave it — keep the two sets separate in your bag
  • Everyday clothes for your days in Makkah and Madinah, a light layer for air-conditioned spaces and cool evenings, sleepwear and enough undergarments

3. Footwear:

  • Comfortable sandals or slippers for the long walks between the Haram and your hotel — slip-on styles are easiest to take off and carry at the mosque doors
  • Socks — for cooler floors, evenings and the walks in between
  • Comfortable, well-worn-in walking shoes for Ziyarat days — never brand-new shoes
  • A small shoe bag, so your footwear stays with you inside the Haram

4. Prayer essentials:

  • A light prayer mat for travel and crowded times
  • A tasbih or counter for dhikr
  • A small dua book, and a pocket Qur'an or a Qur'an app on your phone
  • Your phone with the Nusuk app installed — your Umrah and Rawdah permits live in it. Book the Rawdah slot with our Rawdah Permit guide, and set the app up with our Nusuk App Guide

5. Health:

  • Your regular medicines, in their original packaging with a doctor's note, kept in your cabin bag
  • Painkillers
  • Oral rehydration salts — the walking, heat and crowds test every pilgrim
  • Sunscreen
  • Unscented moisturiser and lip balm — unscented, so they stay within the Ihram rules above
  • Plasters and blister plasters, and throat lozenges, are worth the small space they take

6. Money and tech:

  • Some Saudi riyals in cash for taxis, food and small purchases, plus your bank cards. Split your money between your cabin bag, your checked bag and a money belt — never keep it all in one place — and tell your bank you are travelling so your card is not blocked
  • Phone charger and a power bank — power banks travel in the cabin bag only, never in checked luggage
  • A travel adapter: Saudi sockets are Type G, 220V, like the UK — pack a Type G adapter if your plugs are different

7. Day bag:

  • A small backpack for Haram visits — light enough to carry through long hours of worship
  • A laundry bag for worn clothes
  • Zip-lock bags — for toiletries, snacks and keeping small items dry and together
  • A water bottle — you can refill it with Zamzam inside the Haram. Pack dates, nuts and biscuits alongside it for travel days and long waits

8. Zamzam on the way home: Each passenger may take one factory-sealed 5-litre Zamzam box, bought at the official counters at Jeddah or Madinah airports for about SAR 12.50. It travels always as checked baggage — never in the cabin and never packed inside a suitcase. Saudia gives a complimentary sealed pack from Madinah; from Jeddah it flies free but counts inside your checked allowance. Rules vary by airline, so confirm with your own airline before flying.

Before you close the suitcase: two jobs remain. Choose your dates with our Best Time to Perform Umrah guide, and make sure your visa is right with the Umrah Visa 2026 guide.

Quick questions:

  • Can I use scented toiletries during Umrah? Before you enter Ihram and after you leave it, yes. While you are in Ihram, use only unscented products — no scented products are permitted in Ihram.
  • What does a man wear for Ihram? Two unstitched white cloths, with a belt or pouch for essentials — and no stitched clothes while in Ihram. Pack two sets: one to wear and one clean spare.
  • Can I take Zamzam home in my suitcase or cabin bag? No — never inside a suitcase and never in the cabin. One factory-sealed 5-litre box per passenger, bought at the official airport counters for about SAR 12.50, travels as checked baggage only; confirm your own airline's rule before you fly.
