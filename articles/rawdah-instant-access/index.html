Rawdah Instant Access and Waitlist - No Slot? Try This

The Rawdah is the blessed area inside the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, between the Prophet's house and his pulpit. The Prophet, peace be upon him, said: "Between my house and my pulpit there is a garden of the gardens of Paradise." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196). A permit to pray there is free, and it is booked in the Nusuk app. Most guides describe only one way to book it, but Nusuk offers three routes, and they have different rules. Knowing all three is the difference between getting in and going home without a visit.

Route 1 - The scheduled permit: Book a date and time in advance, from home, in the Nusuk app: open Praying in the Noble Rawdah, pick your name, choose a slot, and issue the permit. The scheduled permit can be used once every 365 days per person. If you cannot attend, cancel in advance: if you simply do not turn up, the booking is cancelled for you and only Instant Track is left. Entry is through the southern courtyards opposite Gate 37. Arrive early and follow the time printed on your permit; the visit lasts about 20 minutes.

Route 2 - Instant Access (Instant Track): This is the route most pilgrims do not know about. Once you are near the Prophet's Mosque, open the app and use Instant Track: new slots are released every 20 minutes through the day, subject to capacity at that moment. You can visit more than once a year this way, as long as you are near the mosque and there is space. It was announced by the Saudi Press Agency on 27 January 2025, together with the waitlist. Turn on your phone location, because the app only offers Instant Track when you are close to the mosque. If a slot appears, book it at once and go; it will not wait for you.

Route 3 - The waitlist: When no slot shows for the day you want, do not keep refreshing the page. Register on the waiting list for that day instead. Nusuk will notify you when a place opens, and you confirm your reservation from the notification. A notification is not a guarantee: the place can go to someone faster, so act the moment it arrives. Use the waitlist and Instant Track together while you are in Madinah; one of the two usually opens up.

Why 'no permits available' usually does not mean sold out: Slots are released in batches, not all at once. Pilgrims report new slots appearing through the day and fresh dates opening about a week ahead, so check the app daily rather than once and giving up. This release pattern is reported by pilgrims, not stated by Nusuk, so treat it as a habit to try, not a promise. Keep your scheduled permit as your guaranteed visit, and treat Instant Track and the waitlist as extra chances.

Timings to plan around: Women and men visit at separate times. For women, the windows announced on 20 October 2026 are after Fajr until 11:00 in the morning and after Isha until 2:00 at night; on Friday the morning window ends at 9:00. Timings change by season, so the time on your permit is the one that counts, not any timetable you read online. The app only shows the windows that apply to you.

Book the scheduled permit from home before you travel. Once you are in Madinah, try Instant Track and join the waitlist for extra visits. Never pay anyone for a Rawdah slot: the permit is free, and anyone selling one is running a scam. For the full step-by-step booking, see our scheduled permit guide. Sisters should also read the Rawdah Permit for Women guide for the women's timings and entry route, and anyone new to the app can start with our Nusuk app guide for setup.

Sources: Saudi Press Agency announcement, 27 January 2025; Nusuk platform wording as checked 10 November 2026; women's timings per the General Authority for the Care of the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques, 20 October 2026.
