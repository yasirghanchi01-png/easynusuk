How to Get a Rawdah Permit — Step by Step

The Rawdah — the blessed area inside the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, between the Prophet's ﷺ house and his pulpit — is entered by permit only. The permit is completely free, and for visitors from outside Saudi Arabia it is booked only through the official Nusuk app. The Tawakalna app serves Saudi citizens and residents; if you are travelling in for Umrah, Nusuk is your route. No agent can book the permit for you, and nobody should charge you for it.

Why pilgrims long for the Rawdah: The Prophet ﷺ said: "Between my house and my pulpit is a garden from the gardens of Paradise" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196; Sahih Muslim 1391).

How to book — step by step:

  • Step 1 — Download the official Nusuk app, and check the publisher is the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah.
  • Step 2 — Register with your passport number and your visa number and phone, exactly as they appear on your documents. Our Nusuk App Guide walks through registration screen by screen.
  • Step 3 — On the Home screen, tap Rawdah.
  • Step 4 — Choose Men or Women. The two have separate visiting times, and a permit booked under the wrong category will not scan at the gate.
  • Step 5 — Pick a green (available) date and time slot — greyed-out slots are full.
  • Step 6 — Confirm the booking.
  • Step 7 — Save the permit and screenshot the QR code. Signal inside the mosque can be weak, and the screenshot opens the gate when the app will not load.
  • Step 8 — On the day, arrive 20–30 minutes early and join the queue for your slot.

One permit a year — and the Instant Track: Under the rule announced in December 2025 by the authority that runs the Two Holy Mosques, each person can hold one advance Rawdah permit every 365 days. If you are already in Madinah, the Nusuk Instant Track can offer same-day slots when you are near the Prophet's Mosque, subject to capacity on the day.

When slots are released: New slots appear in batches, typically on Thursday and Friday mornings around Fajr (Madinah time), and only 1–2 weeks ahead. They go quickly — once your Madinah dates are close, check the app regularly, especially on those mornings.

Confirm, cancel, and how long you get: You must confirm your attendance in the app between 48 hours and 2 hours before the visit. If you cannot attend, cancel in the app — a missed appointment can block future bookings. Men and women visit in separate periods (the Saudi Press Agency published the current visiting periods on 20 October 2026), and a visit lasts about 20–45 minutes, so prepare your duas before you go in. While you are in the city, our Madinah Ziyarat Guide plans the rest of your days.

Quick questions:

  • Is the Rawdah permit free? Yes — completely free, and for international visitors it is issued only through the official Nusuk app. Tawakalna is for Saudi citizens and residents.
  • How often can I book? One advance permit every 365 days under the December 2025 rule. Once you are in Madinah, the Instant Track may offer a same-day slot when you are near the mosque, subject to capacity.
  • What happens if I cannot attend? Confirm your attendance between 48 hours and 2 hours before the visit, and cancel in the app if you cannot go — missed appointments can block future bookings.
