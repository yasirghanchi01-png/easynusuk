Rawdah Permit for Women — Rules, Timings & How to Book

The Rawdah is the most sought after place of prayer in Madinah: the small, blessed area inside the Prophet's Mosque between what was the Prophet's house and his pulpit. Entry is by permit only, the permit is free, and women have their own timings, entry route and booking details. This guide covers the women's side of the visit; for the rules that apply to everyone, see our main Rawdah Permit guide.

Why the Rawdah matters: The Prophet ﷺ said: "Between my house and my pulpit there is a garden of the gardens of Paradise" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196).

Women's visiting times: According to the schedule published by the Saudi Press Agency on 20 October 2026, women may visit the Rawdah from Saturday to Thursday after Fajr prayer until 11:00 AM, and again after Isha prayer until 2:00 AM. On Fridays the morning window is shorter: after Fajr until 9:00 AM, and after Isha until 2:00 AM. These times can change with the season and crowd levels, so treat them as a guide only. The time that counts is the one shown on your permit in the Nusuk app.

How to book, step by step:

  • Step 1: Open the official Nusuk app and sign in with your passport and visa details.
  • Step 2: Open the Rawdah section and choose the women's Rawdah Prayer Permit.
  • Step 3: Pick the date and time slot that fits your stay, and add your companions for your group.
  • Step 4: Confirm the booking. The permit is completely free, and only Nusuk issues it.
  • Step 5: Confirm your attendance in the app between 48 hours and 2 hours before your slot. If you miss this step, the booking can be lost.
  • Step 6: Your QR code appears in the app about 2 hours before the visit. Screenshot it, because the signal inside the mosque can be weak.
  • Step 7: Arrive at least 30 minutes early and follow the signs to the women's entry through the southern courtyards near Gate 37, where staff scan your code.

One visit a year, plus two backups: The scheduled Rawdah permit can be booked only once every 365 days, so choose your slot with care. If that permit is already used, or no slot is left, Nusuk offers two more routes. The Instant Track is for visitors already near the Prophet's Mosque; it opens inside the app when capacity allows. There is also a waitlist: join it and you may be offered a place when another pilgrim cancels.

Practical tips for women:

  • Wear a full, loose abaya and hijab for the visit.
  • Children are not permitted inside the Rawdah, so arrange for someone to look after them before your slot.
  • Your stay inside is short, about 10 to 15 minutes. Decide your duas before you go in, so the time is well spent.
  • You may book for a group of up to 4 companions.
  • New slots are released in batches, often on Fridays, and cancellations appear at any time. Check the app often rather than once.

If no slot shows: Do not give up after one look, and never pay an agent who promises you a permit; the permit is free and only Nusuk issues it. Keep checking the app, especially on Fridays, join the waitlist, and try the Instant Track once you are in Madinah. If the Rawdah does not work out, the city is still full of blessed places, as our Madinah Ziyarat guide shows, and sisters travelling without a mahram will find our Women Travelling Alone article helpful.
