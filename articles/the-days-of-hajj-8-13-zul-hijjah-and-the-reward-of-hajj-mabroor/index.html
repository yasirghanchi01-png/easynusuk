The Days of Hajj, 8–13 Zul-Hijjah — and the Reward of Hajj Mabroor

The Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah orders the Hajj journey as: Mina on the Day of Tarwiyah (8th), then Arafat (9th), Muzdalifah (night), Jamrat al-Aqabah, the sacrifice, Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i (10th), the nights of Tashreeq in Mina (11th–13th), and finally Tawaf al-Wida. The pillars of Hajj are Ihram, the standing at Arafat, Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i.

The heart of it: the Prophet ﷺ said "Hajj is Arafat" (Tirmidhi) — the standing at Arafat on 9 Zul-Hijjah is the rite that cannot be missed. And he ﷺ said: "Umrah to the next Umrah is an expiation for what is between them, and the reward of Hajj Mabroor (an accepted Hajj) is nothing but Paradise" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773; Sahih Muslim 1349). Scholars explain Mabroor as a Hajj performed correctly, sincerely, free of sin and showing-off.
  • 10th: 7 pebbles at Jamrat al-Aqabah only, then sacrifice, then Halq/Taqsir, then Tawaf-uz-Ziyarat
  • 11th–12th: all three Jamarat, 7 pebbles each, after midday; leaving Mina before sunset on the 12th is permitted, otherwise stay for the 13th
