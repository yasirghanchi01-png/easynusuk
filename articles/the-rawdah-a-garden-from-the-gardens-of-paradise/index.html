The Rawdah: A Garden from the Gardens of Paradise

Inside Masjid an-Nabawi in Madinah, between the Prophet's ﷺ chamber and his minbar, lies the Rawdah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "The area between my house and my minbar is one of the gardens of Paradise, and my minbar is on my Cistern (Hawd)" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1196; Sahih Muslim 1391).

Prayer anywhere in Masjid an-Nabawi carries its own immense reward: "One prayer in this mosque of mine is better than a thousand prayers in any other mosque, except Masjid al-Haram" (Bukhari & Muslim). Salamah ibn al-Akwa' (RA) used to seek out prayer in the Rawdah because he had seen the Prophet ﷺ keen to pray there (Bukhari 502; Muslim 509).

  • Book your Rawdah slot in the official Nusuk app — slots fill within minutes
  • Inside: pray 2 rakat calmly, send salutations, and make dua — without pushing or troubling anyone; eagerness for the Rawdah never justifies harming another worshipper
  • Afterwards, present your salam at the Sacred Chamber, then greet Abu Bakr (RA) and Umar (RA)

Sahih al-Bukhari 1196, 502 · Sahih Muslim 1391, 509 · Ar-Rawdah an-Nabawiyah ↗
