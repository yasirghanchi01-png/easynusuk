Umrah Dua Book — Essential Duas for the Whole Journey

One page to save or print before you travel: the essential duas of Umrah, from leaving home to coming back, each with its Arabic, transliteration and meaning — so you are never stuck for words at the moments that matter most.

1. Before travel — boarding the plane: Say Allahu Akbar three times, then recite the verse the Prophet ﷺ recited when setting out on a journey:

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَى رَبِّنَا لَمُنْقَلِبُونَ

"Subhanal-ladhi sakhkhara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun"

"Glory be to Him who has made this subservient to us, for we could never have done it ourselves, and to our Lord we will surely return." (Qur'an 43:13–14, recited by the Prophet ﷺ when setting out — Sahih Muslim 1342)

Then continue with the rest of the travel dua: "Allahumma inna nas'aluka fi safarina hadhal-birra wat-taqwa, wa minal-'amali ma tarda. Allahumma hawwin 'alayna safarana hadha, watwi 'anna bu'dah. Allahumma antas-sahibu fis-safar, wal-khalifatu fil-ahl. Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min wa'tha'is-safar, wa ka'abatil-manzar, wa su'il-munqalabi fil-mali wal-ahl." — "O Allah, on this journey of ours we ask You for righteousness and piety, and for deeds that please You. O Allah, make this journey easy for us and shorten its distance. O Allah, You are our Companion on the journey and the One who looks after our family. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the hardship of travel, from distressing sights, and from returning to find harm in our property or family." (Sahih Muslim 1342)

2. Entering Makkah:

اللَّهُمَّ هَذَا حَرَمُكَ وَأَمْنُكَ، فَحَرِّمْنِي عَلَى النَّارِ، وَأَمِّنِّي مِنْ عَذَابِكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ، وَاجْعَلْنِي مِنْ أَوْلِيَائِكَ وَأَهْلِ طَاعَتِكَ

"Allahumma hadha haramuka wa amnuka, fa-harrimni 'alan-nar, wa amminni min 'adhabika yawma tab'athu 'ibadak, waj'alni min awliya'ika wa ahli ta'atik"

"O Allah, this is Your Sanctuary and place of safety. Forbid me from the Fire, protect me from Your punishment on the Day You raise Your servants, and make me of Your friends and the people of Your obedience." (Musannaf Ibn Abi Shaybah 7549; al-Bayhaqi, Shu'ab al-Iman 3969)

3. First sight of the Kaaba:

اللَّهُمَّ زِدْ هَذَا الْبَيْتَ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَمَهَابَةً، وَزِدْ مَنْ شَرَّفَهُ وَكَرَّمَهُ مِمَّنْ حَجَّهُ أَوِ اعْتَمَرَهُ تَشْرِيفًا وَتَكْرِيمًا وَتَعْظِيمًا وَبِرًّا

"Allahumma zid hadhal-bayta tashrifan wa ta'dhiman wa takriman wa mahabah, wa zid man sharrafahu wa karramahu mimman hajjahu awi'tamarahu tashrifan wa takriman wa ta'dhiman wa birra"

"O Allah, increase this House in honour, greatness, nobility and awe. And increase those who honour and revere it, among those who perform Hajj or Umrah to it, in honour, nobility, greatness and righteousness." (al-Bayhaqi, Sunan 5/73; Ibn Khuzaymah 4/212, graded Hasan)

Scholars say the first sight of the Kaaba is a moment when dua is accepted — raise your hands and ask for whatever is in your heart. If you cannot remember the wording, say Allahu Akbar three times and make your own personal dua.

4. During Tawaf — between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone:

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

"Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar"

"Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire." (Qur'an 2:201)

The Prophet ﷺ said this dua between the two corners (Sunan Abi Dawud 1892, graded Hasan by al-Albani), and it was also his most frequent dua (Sahih al-Bukhari 6389). For the full set of Tawaf supplications, see our Tawaf Duas article and the Tawaf Guide.

5. At Safa and Marwah (Sa'i): On first approaching Safa, recite this verse once:

إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِنْ شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ

"Innas-Safa wal-Marwata min sha'a'irillah"

"Indeed, Safa and Marwah are among the symbols of Allah" (Qur'an 2:158) — then say "Abda'u bima bada'allahu bihi" ("I begin with what Allah began with").

Then, on top of each hill, facing the Kaaba, say Allahu Akbar three times, then say the following three times, making your own personal dua between each repetition:

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ. لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ

"La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir. La ilaha illallahu wahdahu, anjaza wa'dah, wa nasara 'abdah, wa hazama al-ahzaba wahdah"

"None has the right to be worshipped except Allah, alone, without a partner. To Him belongs all sovereignty and praise. He is over all things omnipotent. None has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, aided His slave, and single-handedly defeated the confederates." (Sahih Muslim 1218)

There is no fixed dua for the walk between the hills — ask Allah for anything from your heart as you walk. Our Sa'i Guide walks through the rite step by step.

6. Drinking Zamzam:

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ

"Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an wa rizqan wasi'an wa shifa'an min kulli da'"

"O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant provision, and healing from every disease." (Reported from Ibn Abbas (RA) — al-Hakim, al-Mustadrak 1739; Sunan al-Daraqutni 2738; Musannaf 'Abd al-Razzaq 9112, with a good chain)

Remember the hadith: "The water of Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for" (Sunan Ibn Majah 3062). Face the qiblah, say Bismillah, and drink in three sips. The full story of the well is in our Zamzam: Virtues and History article.

7. Leaving Makkah — returning home:

آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ لِرَبِّنَا حَامِدُونَ

"Ayibuna ta'ibuna 'abiduna li-rabbina hamiduna"

"We return, repenting, worshipping, and praising our Lord." (Sahih Muslim 1342 — the Prophet's ﷺ dua on returning from a journey)

Practical tip: Save this page offline or print it before you travel — phone signal inside the Haram can be weak. For everything else you need to carry, see our Umrah Packing List, and if this is your first journey, our Umrah for First-Timers guide walks through the whole trip in order.

Quick questions:

  • Do I have to memorise all of these? No. Only the Tawaf dua between the corners and the Sa'i dhikr are specifically reported; for everything else, sincere personal dua in your own words is accepted.
  • Can I read duas from my phone during Tawaf? Yes, many pilgrims do. Keep your focus, and do not block others.
  • Is there a fixed dua for each round of Tawaf? No — except the Rabbana atina dua between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, which is said on every round.
