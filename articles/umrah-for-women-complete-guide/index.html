Umrah for Women — Complete Guide

Umrah is a journey of a lifetime for a woman just as it is for a man, and most of its rites are exactly the same. Allah says: "And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah" (Qur'an 2:196). But there are a few rules that apply to women only — in Ihram, in Tawaf and Sa'i, and when the monthly period comes. This guide brings them together in one place.

Ihram for women: A woman enters Ihram in her normal, modest clothes — the abaya and hijab she usually wears. There is no special Ihram garment for women, and ordinary stitched clothing is allowed for her. Two things are forbidden for her in Ihram: the niqab (face veil) and gloves.

From the Sunnah: the Prophet ﷺ said: "A woman in Ihram should not wear a niqab, nor wear gloves" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1838). When non-mahram men pass by, she may lower her headscarf over her face to cover it — this is what the wives of the Prophet ﷺ used to do.

Travelling without a mahram: Saudi Arabia changed the rule in 2022: a woman aged 18 or over may now perform Umrah without a mahram. A girl under 18 still travels with a mahram or guardian. At every age, the Umrah visa and Umrah permit are booked through the Nusuk app, the same as for every other pilgrim.

How a woman's rites differ from a man's:

  • No Ramal — the brisk walk men do in the first three circuits of Tawaf. A woman walks at a normal pace in all seven circuits.
  • No Idtiba — baring the right shoulder is for men only; a woman's shoulder stays covered.
  • A normal pace in Sa'i too — between the two green lights men walk briskly; women walk normally throughout Sa'i.
  • The Talbiyah in a low voice — a woman recites "Labbayk Allahumma labbayk..." quietly, loud enough for herself to hear.
  • Ending Ihram: trim, do not shave — after Sa'i, a woman trims about a fingertip's length (1–2 cm) from the ends of her hair. With this, her Ihram ends and her Umrah is complete.

Menstruation during Umrah: A woman cannot perform Tawaf while menstruating. She waits in Makkah until she is pure, performs ghusl, and only then does her Tawaf. She may still enter Ihram at the Miqat while menstruating, and her Ihram stays valid while she waits — there is no need to panic or to cut the trip short.

From the Sunnah: when Aisha (RA) menstruated during the journey, the Prophet ﷺ told her: "Do everything that the pilgrims do, but do not circumambulate the House until you become pure" (Sahih al-Bukhari 1650; Sahih Muslim 1211).

Sa'i is different: Sa'i does not require wudu. So if a woman's period begins after she has completed her Tawaf, she may carry on with Sa'i, then trim her hair and finish her Umrah.

Visiting the Rawdah in Madinah: Women have their own separate visiting times at the Rawdah in the Prophet's Mosque — usually in the morning after Fajr and again at night after Isha. Entry is by permit only: book "Praying in the Noble Rawdah" and choose the women's service in the Nusuk app, then arrive at the gate shown on your permit 20–30 minutes early.

Practical tips:

  • Use the women's prayer areas — both Harams have large, clearly marked sections for women; in Makkah there are women's areas close to the Mataf, and mosque staff will guide you to them.
  • Pack for the journey: comfortable, well-worn-in shoes for the long walks; a spare abaya and extra hijabs; and unscented toiletries — no perfume, scented soap or scented wipes while in Ihram.
  • Travel in a group if you can — a family group or a women's group makes the journey easier and more relaxed, especially on a first Umrah.
  • Choose a hotel near the Haram — a short walk saves your energy for worship, above all when returning for Fajr.
  • Wheelchair assistance is available — wheelchairs can be arranged in both Harams for Tawaf and Sa'i if you need one; ask the staff at the mosque entrances.

Quick questions:

  • Can a woman perform Umrah without a mahram? Yes. Since 2022, Saudi rules allow women aged 18 and over to perform Umrah without a mahram; under 18, she travels with a mahram or guardian.
  • What if my period starts before Tawaf? Wait until you are pure, perform ghusl, and then do Tawaf. Your Ihram remains valid while you wait.
  • How does a woman come out of Ihram? By trimming about a fingertip's length (1–2 cm) from the ends of her hair after Sa'i — she does not shave her head.
