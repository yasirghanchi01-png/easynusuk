Umrah from Australia — Visa & Flights Guide

Australian pilgrims have one of the simplest visa routes to Umrah: Australia is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, so you can apply online before you fly or pick up a visa on arrival, and still use the dedicated Umrah visa route when you prefer a package. The journey itself is the long part — there are no direct flights, and the fastest trips take about 19 hours. This guide covers both visa routes, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules, and Sydney, Melbourne and Perth flights and fares. The same eVisa route from the Southern Hemisphere is covered in our companion guide, Umrah from South Africa, and for Asia in Umrah from Malaysia and Umrah from Turkey, while our Umrah Visa for UK & US Citizens guide walks through the same online application step by step.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Umrah to Umrah expiates what is between them." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773, Sahih Muslim 1349)

Do Australians need a visa for Umrah? Yes — but you can get yours online in minutes or at the airport. Australia is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, and the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk remains available when you prefer everything arranged in a package. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out how the fees compare across the visa types.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa (or visa on arrival): Apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com. The eVisa costs SAR 535 in total, including the mandatory health insurance, is valid for 1 year with multiple entries, and allows up to 90 days per visit (90 days total per year). Umrah is allowed on it outside the Hajj season. A visa on arrival is available on the same terms at Saudi airports, including at the self-service kiosks. Note that travellers under 18 must be accompanied by a parent, grandparent or adult sibling over 18.

Route 2: the Umrah visa via Nusuk or an agent: Apply on the Nusuk Umrah platform or through a licensed Australian Umrah agent. Since July 2026 there is also a multiple-entry Umrah visa: valid for 365 days with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days, and inactive during the Hajj season. Our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup and the permit booking step by step.

A route that does not exist — the Package eVisa: The Package eVisa is not available for Australia. It covers only seven countries — Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan — so do not plan around it.

The 1448H season dates:

  • Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • Pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • ACWY meningococcal vaccination is mandatory, at least 10 days before travel, with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Australia.
  • A yellow fever certificate is needed only if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram.

Flights from Australia to Jeddah: There are no direct flights from any Australian city to Jeddah — every option is 1-stop, over a distance of about 13,302 km. The fastest 1-stop options take about 19 hours: via Abu Dhabi with Etihad (about 19h 15m), via Dubai with Emirates (shortest 1-stop average about 19h 01m), via Singapore with Singapore Airlines (about 19h 55m), or via Doha with Qatar Airways (about 20h 09m). Departures are from Sydney and Melbourne; Perth connects through the same hubs. One-way fares start from about AU$790; returns start from about AU$1,674, typically AU$1,678–AU$2,394 (KAYAK, November 2026), with Etihad from AU$2,184.92. Book about 10 weeks ahead for the best prices — November is usually the cheapest month. All fares checked 19 Nov 2026, prices change daily. Jeddah is 7–8 hours behind Sydney, depending on Australian daylight saving (AEST/AEDT against AST UTC+3), so allow a gentle first day after landing. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and if your stay splits between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi, while our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: On the eVisa route you pay the SAR 535 visa fee yourself and book flights and hotels separately; on the Umrah visa route the visa comes inside the package. Flights are the biggest variable on a trip this long, then hotels near the Haram. See our general Umrah cost guide and the Makkah hotels guide before you book.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — tourist eVisa online or on arrival, or the Umrah visa via Nusuk or a licensed agent.
  3. Get the ACWY vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your flights and hotels (or your service package on the Umrah visa route).
  5. Get your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. A 19-hour journey rewards planning: choose your dates with the best time to perform Umrah guide, which compares weather, crowds and cost across the year, and if you are travelling as a family — remembering that under-18s must be accompanied by a parent, grandparent or adult sibling — our Umrah with Kids guide covers prams, pacing and child-friendly hotels.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Is Australia on the Saudi tourist eVisa list? Yes. Australians can apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com for SAR 535 including mandatory health insurance (1 year, multiple entry, up to 90 days per visit), or get a visa on arrival on the same terms, and perform Umrah outside the Hajj season.
  • Are there direct flights from Australia to Jeddah? No — there are no direct flights from any Australian city. The fastest options are 1-stop at about 19 hours, via Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Singapore or Doha.
  • Can travellers under 18 fly in on a visa on arrival alone? No. Travellers under 18 must be accompanied by a parent, grandparent or adult sibling over 18.
  • Is the Package eVisa available for Australia? No. The Package eVisa covers only Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.
