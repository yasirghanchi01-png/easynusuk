Umrah from Czech Republic — Visa & Flights Guide

Czech pilgrims have one of the easiest Umrah routes in Europe: the Czech Republic (Czechia) is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, so you can apply for a tourist eVisa online before you fly, get a visa on arrival, or still use the dedicated Umrah visa route when you prefer a package. The Czech Republic is an EU member and a Schengen member. There are no direct flights from Prague to Jeddah in the current schedule, so every route to Jeddah is one stop. This guide covers both visa routes, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules, and the flights and typical journey times. Travelling from elsewhere in Europe? Our newest companion guide, Umrah from Slovakia, covers the same eVisa route for travellers from Slovakia, and our Umrah from Poland and Umrah from Hungary guides cover it for travellers from Poland and Hungary.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Umrah to Umrah expiates what is between them." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773, Sahih Muslim 1349)

Do Czech pilgrims need a visa for Umrah? Yes — but you can get yours online in advance or at the airport. The Czech Republic is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, and the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk remains available when you prefer everything arranged in a package. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out how the fees compare across the visa types.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa (or visa on arrival): Apply online at the official portal visa.visitsaudi.com. The eVisa costs SAR 535 in total, including the mandatory medical insurance (about US$143). It is valid for 1 year with multiple entries and allows up to 90 days per visit. Your passport must be valid for at least six months from entry, and most applications are approved within minutes to 24 hours. Umrah is allowed on it outside the Hajj season — book the Umrah permit on the Nusuk app — and it is not valid for Hajj or work. A visa on arrival is available on the same terms for Czech passport holders at Saudi international airports.

Route 2: the Umrah visa via Nusuk or an agent: Apply through the Nusuk app or a licensed travel agent, and enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the visa being issued. Since 20 July 2026 there is also a multiple-entry Umrah visa: valid for 365 days with up to 90 days of cumulative stay — each visit needs its own Nusuk service package and Umrah permit — and it is not usable during the Hajj season. Our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup and the permit booking step by step.

The 1448H season dates:

  • Umrah visas for the 1448H season have been issued since 31 May 2026.
  • Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • All Umrah visa holders must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • Meningococcal ACWY vaccination is mandatory, at least 10 days before arrival — the conjugate vaccine is valid for 5 years and the polysaccharide vaccine for 3 years — with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from the Czech Republic.
  • A yellow fever certificate is needed only if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram — our Umrah for women travelling alone guide has the rules and practical detail.

Flights from the Czech Republic to Jeddah: There are no direct flights from Prague (PRG) to Jeddah (JED) in the current schedule window (checked 23 Nov 2026). The fastest one-stop option takes about 8h 55m: Pegasus Airlines via Istanbul Sabiha (SAW) — for example, departing Prague at 14:10 and arriving in Jeddah at 01:05. Turkish Airlines via Istanbul (IST) takes about 9 to 10 hours in total, and AJet via Istanbul Sabiha about 10h 25m to 11h 20m; one-stop routes also run via Doha (Qatar Airways), Dubai (Emirates) or Rome. The cheapest one-way fares start at around 15,774 INR (about US$178) on Trip.com for January dates — fares change, so check before booking. The Prague–Jeddah distance is about 3,837 km. You fly from Prague Václav Havel Airport (PRG) into Jeddah King Abdulaziz International Airport (JED), and from the airport the Haramain high-speed train reaches Makkah in about 45 minutes. Journey times and fares are indicative, checked 23 Nov 2026 — check live schedules and prices before booking. The Czech Republic uses the Czech koruna (CZK), and cards are widely accepted in Saudi Arabia. Prague keeps CET (UTC+1) in winter and CEST (UTC+2) in summer, so Jeddah is 2 hours ahead of Prague in winter and 1 hour ahead in summer, while Jeddah stays on AST (UTC+3) all year — once you land, set your prayer alarms by the local Makkah and Madinah times in this site's Namaz section. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and if your stay splits between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi, while our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: On the eVisa route you pay the SAR 535 visa fee yourself and book flights and hotels separately; on the Umrah visa route the visa comes inside the package. Flights are the biggest variable, then hotels near the Haram. See our Makkah hotels guide before you book, and add up the full budget — visa, flights, hotels, food and local transport — before you commit.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — tourist eVisa online or on arrival, or the Umrah visa via Nusuk or a licensed Czech travel agent.
  3. Get the meningococcal ACWY vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your flights and hotels (or your package on the Umrah visa route).
  5. Book the free Umrah permit on the Nusuk app before entering Masjid al-Haram.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

A short practical checklist: Install the Nusuk app and book your Umrah permit before you fly; book the Rawdah permit as soon as your Madinah dates are fixed, because slots go quickly; pack your Ihram garments on top of your bag and read our Ihram rules guide so you are ready to enter Ihram before the miqat announcement on board; drink Zamzam freely in Makkah and buy a sealed bottle at Jeddah airport for the flight home instead of packing loose bottles; and work through our Umrah packing list so nothing essential is left behind.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. Choose your dates with the best time to perform Umrah guide, which compares weather, crowds and cost across the year, and learn the supplications from Tawaf Duas and the wider Umrah Duas collection before you fly, so the rites themselves feel familiar when you reach the Haram.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Is the Czech Republic on the Saudi tourist eVisa list? Yes — the Czech Republic (Czechia) is on the official list at visa.visitsaudi.com (checked 23 November 2026). Apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com.
  • Can I perform Umrah on the tourist eVisa? Yes, outside the Hajj season, with the Umrah permit booked on Nusuk. The eVisa costs SAR 535 including mandatory health insurance (about US$143), lasts 1 year with multiple entries, and allows up to 90 days per visit; a visa on arrival is available on the same terms for Czech passport holders. It is not valid for Hajj or work.
  • Are there direct flights from the Czech Republic to Jeddah? No — there are no direct Prague to Jeddah flights in the current schedule. Every route is one stop: the fastest is Pegasus Airlines via Istanbul Sabiha at about 8h 55m, Turkish Airlines via Istanbul takes about 9 to 10 hours, and one-stop routes also run via Doha, Dubai or Rome.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and all Umrah visa holders must depart by 7 April 2027.
