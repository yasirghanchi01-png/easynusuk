Umrah from Egypt — Visa & Flights Guide

Egypt sends one of the largest Umrah contingents in the world, and Cairo to Jeddah is one of the shortest flights to the Haramain anywhere — a little over two hours in the air. The visa, though, is where Egyptian pilgrims must be careful. Egypt is not among the nationalities eligible for the Saudi tourist eVisa, and there is no visa on arrival on an ordinary Egyptian passport. For almost every Egyptian pilgrim the visa is a dedicated Umrah visa, arranged through the Nusuk platform or a licensed Egyptian company, which also registers your programme on the Egyptian Umrah portal and issues the Egyptian Umrah barcode. This guide covers the visa routes that genuinely exist in the 1448 AH season, the one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa introduced in July 2026, the documents and vaccinations you need, and Cairo–Jeddah flights and fares. Egyptian pilgrims travel under the same broad system as readers of our other non-eVisa country guides — compare Umrah from Nigeria, Umrah from Kenya and Umrah from Indonesia, and our Asian guides to Umrah from Pakistan, Umrah from India and Umrah from Bangladesh.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Egyptians need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Every Egyptian passport holder needs a valid Saudi visa before travelling. When Saudi Arabia expanded its tourist eVisa list on 5 November 2026, the list grew to 72 eligible nationalities — and Egypt is not among them (VisaEntryRules; House of Saud; Travelobiz). Some older pages still quote a figure of 66 eligible nationalities; that number is out of date, but either way Egypt is not on the list, and there is no visa on arrival for Egyptians travelling on an ordinary passport alone. Anyone who offers you a tourist eVisa on an Egyptian passport alone, without the exception in Route 1 below, is mistaken. Note also that the Egypt–Saudi reciprocal short-stay visa exemption signed on 19 March 2026 covers holders of diplomatic, special and service passports only — it does not apply to ordinary Egyptian passports (Saudi Arabia Immigration).

Route 1: the tourist eVisa exception (only if you qualify): An Egyptian passport holder can still apply for the Saudi tourist eVisa if you hold a valid, previously used US, UK or Schengen visa, or you are a permanent resident of the EU, the UK, the US or a GCC state (VisaEntryRules; Saudi Arabia Immigration). Umrah may be performed on the tourist eVisa outside the Hajj season, with an Umrah permit booked in the Nusuk app, and our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out the wider fee picture.

Route 2: the dedicated Umrah visa (the route almost every Egyptian uses): The standard route is a dedicated Umrah visa arranged through the Nusuk platform or a licensed Egyptian Umrah company. Since August 2025, pilgrims can apply directly on Nusuk and book their accommodation, transport and tours on the platform itself. If you book through a company, the licensed company registers your programme on the Egyptian Umrah portal and issues the Egyptian Umrah barcode that Egyptian pilgrims need before departure (WowTrek). Note also that seasonal programme payments in Egypt are made in Egyptian pounds, not in dollars or riyals (WowTrek). Whichever route you use, you still book your Umrah permit and your Rawdah permit yourself in the Nusuk app — our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup step by step.

The one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa (since July 2026): Since July 2026 the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah has issued a one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa: it is valid for 365 days from the date of issue, with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days shared across all your trips in that year. You must obtain an Umrah permit through Nusuk before each arrival, and the visa does not activate during the Hajj season — from 1 Dhu al-Qi'dah to 13 Dhu al-Hijjah (VisaEntryRules; WowTrek). The 90 days are a yearly total — they do not reset on each entry.

Single-entry rules and the 1448 AH season:

  • Since February 2025, Egypt has been one of fourteen nationalities limited to single-entry visit visas — but that restriction does not affect Umrah or Hajj visas, so it does not limit your Umrah options (VisaEntryRules).
  • The last day for Umrah visa issuance in the 1448 AH season is 9 March 2027.
  • The last day to enter Saudi Arabia on an Umrah visa is 23 March 2027.
  • Every Umrah visitor must depart by 7 April 2027, before the Hajj season closes the Haram to Umrah pilgrims.

Documents and vaccinations checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date.
  • Meningococcal ACWY vaccination — mandatory for every pilgrim, with the certificate issued at least 10 days before arrival. Carry the certificate in your hand luggage.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Egypt — Saudi Arabia only recommends it, so it is a sensible precaution rather than an entry requirement.
  • Yellow fever vaccination is required only if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country (WowTrek; Umrah Packages Deals).
  • Your verified hotel and transport bookings, arranged through Nusuk or your licensed company, plus a confirmed return ticket.
  • Women of all ages may perform Umrah without a mahram (Umrah Packages Deals).

Flights from Cairo to Jeddah: Jeddah (King Abdulaziz International, JED) is the arrival airport for Makkah: direct flights from Cairo (CAI) take about 2 hours 10 minutes to 2 hours 20 minutes. Eight airlines fly the route — EgyptAir, Saudia, flynas, flyadeal, Air Cairo, Nile Air, Air Arabia Egypt and Nesma Airlines — with Saudia operating about 61 flights a week and EgyptAir about 54 a week (Priceline schedule data). Fares checked on 18 November 2026: a round trip starts from about US$190 (Flighko); one-way fares start from about US$104 (Expedia) and about US$127 (Priceline), with a typical one-way range of US$75–305 (Expedia). All fares checked 18 Nov 2026, prices change daily. If you enter Ihram for the flight, the Miqat is crossed before landing — the crew normally announce it — and our Miqat guide explains the boundaries. Frequently asked questions:

  • Can Egyptians get a Saudi tourist eVisa or visa on arrival? Not on an Egyptian passport alone — Egypt is not among the 72 eligible nationalities after the 5 November 2026 expansion, and there is no visa on arrival. The only eVisa exception is for holders of a used US, UK or Schengen visa, or permanent residents of the EU, UK, US or a GCC state.
  • How do most Egyptians get their Umrah visa? Through a dedicated Umrah visa arranged on the Nusuk platform or by a licensed Egyptian company, which registers the programme on the Egyptian Umrah portal and issues the Egyptian Umrah barcode. Seasonal programme payments are made in Egyptian pounds.
  • Which vaccinations do I need from Egypt? Meningococcal ACWY is mandatory, with the certificate issued at least 10 days before arrival. Polio vaccination is recommended but not compulsory for travellers from Egypt, and yellow fever applies only if you arrive from or transit a yellow-fever risk country.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448 AH? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and all Umrah visitors must depart by 7 April 2027.
