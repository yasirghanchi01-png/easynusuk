Umrah from Germany — Visa & Flights Guide

German pilgrims have one of the easiest Umrah routes in Europe: Germany is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list — on the Europe list (Wego, updated 12 Nov 2026) — and Saudia flies non-stop from Frankfurt to Jeddah. That means you can apply for a tourist eVisa online before you fly, get a visa on arrival, or still use the dedicated Umrah visa route when you prefer a package. This guide covers both routes, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules, and Frankfurt flights and fares. Travelling from across the Channel instead? Our newest companion guide, Umrah from United Kingdom, covers the same eVisa route with a daily non-stop London flight, and our Umrah Visa for UK & US Citizens guide walks through the same online application step by step.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Umrah to Umrah expiates what is between them." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773, Sahih Muslim 1349)

Do German pilgrims need a visa for Umrah? Yes — but you can get yours online in advance or at the airport. Germany is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, and the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk remains available when you prefer everything arranged in a package. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out how the fees compare across the visa types.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa (or visa on arrival): Apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com. The eVisa costs SAR 535 in total, including the mandatory health insurance (about €130), is valid for 1 year with multiple entries, and allows up to 90 days per visit. Umrah is allowed on it outside the Hajj season; it is not valid for Hajj or work. A visa on arrival is available on the same terms for German passport holders at Saudi airports.

Route 2: the Umrah visa via Nusuk or an agent: Apply on the Nusuk app or through a licensed German travel agent. Since July 2026 there is also a multiple-entry Umrah visa: valid for 365 days with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days, and inactive during the Hajj season. Our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup and the permit booking step by step.

A route that does not exist — the Package eVisa: The Package eVisa is not available for Germany. It covers only seven countries — Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan — so do not plan around it.

The 1448H season dates:

  • Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • Pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • ACWY meningitis vaccination is mandatory, at least 10 days before travel, with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Germany.
  • A yellow fever certificate is needed only if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram — our Umrah for women travelling alone guide has the rules and practical detail.

Flights from Germany to Jeddah: Saudia is the only non-stop operator from Frankfurt to Jeddah, taking about 5h 45m and flying four days a week — Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday — over a distance of 2,567 miles (about 4,132 km). Lufthansa flies direct from Frankfurt to Riyadh (about 5h 30m), not Jeddah, so for Jeddah, Saudia is the non-stop choice. One-stop options run via Doha, Dubai, Istanbul or Cairo. The cheapest non-stop fares start from about $722 (about €625), and typical returns run $688–$856 (about €620–€770). All fares checked 19 Nov 2026, prices change daily. Jeddah is 2 hours ahead of Germany in winter (CET) and 1 hour ahead in summer (CEST). On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and if your stay splits between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi, while our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: On the eVisa route you pay the SAR 535 visa fee yourself and book flights and hotels separately; on the Umrah visa route the visa comes inside the package. Flights are the biggest variable, then hotels near the Haram. See our general Umrah cost guide and the Makkah hotels guide before you book.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — tourist eVisa online or on arrival, or the Umrah visa via Nusuk or a licensed German travel agent.
  3. Get the ACWY meningitis vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your flights and hotels (or your package on the Umrah visa route).
  5. Book the free Umrah permit on the Nusuk app before entering Masjid al-Haram.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. Choose your dates with the best time to perform Umrah guide, which compares weather, crowds and cost across the year, and learn the supplications from Umrah Duas and Tawaf Duas before you fly, so the rites themselves feel familiar when you reach the Haram.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Is Germany on the Saudi tourist eVisa list? Yes — it is on the Europe list (Wego, updated 12 Nov 2026). Apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com.
  • Can I perform Umrah on the tourist eVisa? Yes, outside the Hajj season. The eVisa costs SAR 535 including mandatory health insurance (about €130), lasts 1 year with multiple entries, and allows up to 90 days per visit; a visa on arrival is available on the same terms for German passport holders. It is not valid for Hajj or work.
  • Is there a direct flight from Germany to Jeddah? Yes — Saudia is the only non-stop operator from Frankfurt to Jeddah, about 5h 45m, four days a week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday). Lufthansa flies direct to Riyadh, not Jeddah.
  • Is the Package eVisa available for Germany? No. The Package eVisa covers only Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.
