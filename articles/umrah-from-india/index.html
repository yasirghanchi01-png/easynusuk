Umrah from India — Visa & Flights Guide

Planning Umrah from India is straightforward once you know which visa route applies to you. The one fact that surprises most first-time pilgrims is this: India is not on Saudi Arabia's tourist eVisa list, so most Indian passport holders cannot apply for a visa online by themselves. The standard route is a dedicated Umrah visa arranged through an approved agent on the Nusuk platform, with your hotel and transport booked as part of the package. This guide covers all three real routes, the documents you need, flights from Indian cities, and what the trip costs in rupees.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Indian citizens need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Every Indian passport holder needs a valid Saudi visa to enter the Kingdom and perform Umrah. There is no visa-free entry and no visa-on-arrival for Indian citizens travelling on an ordinary Indian passport alone. The Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah confirms that holders of tourist, eVisa and Umrah visas can all perform Umrah while in Saudi Arabia, as long as they secure the Umrah permit through the Nusuk app.

Route 1: Umrah visa through an approved agent (the route most pilgrims use): This is the standard route for Indian passport holders living in India. You book a package through an Umrah agent in India who is approved by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. The agent arranges the visa through the Nusuk system together with your hotel and transport bookings. In 2026, Nusuk hotel and transport bookings are mandatory, and standalone visa-only bookings are not available for Indian passport holders — the visa comes as part of a package.

Key rules for the 1448 AH Umrah season:

  • The visa is valid for 30 days from the date it is issued, reduced from 90 days in earlier seasons. You must enter Saudi Arabia within that 30-day window. Once you enter, you can stay up to 90 days.
  • The 1448 season runs to 7 April 2027. Visa issuance ends on 9 March 2027 and the last entry date is 23 March 2027, so plan your travel dates before you apply.
  • Since July 2026 there is also a one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa, valid for 365 days with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days. Each entry needs its own service package booked through an approved Nusuk provider, plus an Umrah permit in the Nusuk app. It is not valid during the Hajj season.
  • Umrah visas are not issued in the weeks around Hajj.

Before paying any agent, ask for the package in writing: the visa, hotel names and their distance from the Haram, transport, meals if included, and what is not included. A genuine agent will give you all of this without hesitation.

Route 2: Saudi tourist eVisa (only if you qualify): India is not among the 72 nationalities eligible for Saudi Arabia's direct tourist eVisa — that list was expanded on 5 November 2026 and India is still not on it. But an Indian passport holder can still apply for the tourist eVisa if any one of these applies to you:

  • You hold a valid US, UK or Schengen visit visa that you have already used to enter that country.
  • You are a permanent resident of the US, the UK or an EU country.
  • You are a resident of a Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) country.

Apply directly on the official portal, visa.visitsaudi.com. Our Umrah Visa for UK & US Citizens guide walks through the eVisa steps in detail. The tourist eVisa permits Umrah, but not Hajj, and you still need to book your Umrah permit on Nusuk before entering the Haram. Note that since February 2025, Indian nationals have been limited to single-entry visit visas, so do not rely on a family visit visa as your Umrah route — use the dedicated Umrah visa instead.

Route 3: the 96-hour stopover visa (free): If you are flying with Saudia or flynas and transiting through Saudi Arabia, you can get a free 96-hour stopover visa. Within those 96 hours you can perform Umrah, provided you book your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app. This suits pilgrims who are already flying through Jeddah or Riyadh to another destination, but it is a tight window — it is not a substitute for a proper Umrah trip if you want unhurried days in Makkah and Madinah.

What the visa costs: The Saudi government charges SAR 300 for the visa plus SAR 100 to 180 for mandatory health insurance, a total of SAR 400 to 480 before any agent service fee. Indian agents advertise visa-only prices from around ₹14,990 to ₹15,999, with full packages priced separately. Always ask for a written breakdown of the government fee, insurance and the agent's own service charge. See also our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide for the full breakdown by visa type.

Documents checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months from your arrival date, with blank pages.
  • Recent passport-size photograph.
  • Meningococcal meningitis (ACWY) vaccination certificate, taken at least 10 days before arrival. Carry the original certificate with you.
  • Confirmed hotel and transport bookings made through Nusuk by your agent — mandatory in 2026.
  • Confirmed return ticket.
  • Your agent may ask you to complete biometrics in the Saudi Visa Bio app. If so, do it as soon as the agent asks, because the visa cannot be filed until it is done.

Flights from India to Jeddah and Madinah: Direct flights operate from Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad and Lucknow, on Saudia, IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express and Flynas, with one-stop options on Gulf carriers. Mumbai to Jeddah is about four and a half hours nonstop; Delhi to Jeddah is about six hours and forty minutes. You can fly into Jeddah (JED) for Makkah, or into Madinah (MED) if you want to start your trip in Madinah. Once you land, see our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide for taxi, bus and train options and 2026 costs.

Recent one-way economy fares seen in November 2026 checks:

  • Mumbai to Jeddah on IndiGo: median fare ₹26,579; lowest fare seen ₹15,655.
  • Delhi to Jeddah on IndiGo: median fare ₹39,377; best fare found ₹22,325.
  • Mumbai to Jeddah return: typically ₹30,551 to ₹44,609.
  • India to Madinah one-way from ₹17,803; return from ₹37,208.

Fares change daily and rise sharply in Ramadan and school holidays, so treat these as a guide and check live prices for your dates before booking.

Package or DIY? Most first-time pilgrims from India take a package, and on the Umrah visa route the package is effectively how the visa is issued. Advertised November 2026 package prices from Indian operators: economy 15 to 17-day packages from ₹99,000 per person; a 7-day Ramadan package from ₹98,000; deluxe packages around ₹1,24,000; and super-deluxe five-star packages around ₹1,45,000. All are per person on a sharing basis and include return flights, visa, hotels, transport and ziyarat. If you qualify for the tourist eVisa route, you can also book everything yourself — flights, hotels and transport — and manage the trip independently.

Best time to go from India: Winter, from November to February, is the most comfortable weather for Umrah, with cooler days and pleasant nights. The quieter weeks after Hajj and in late autumn often have the lowest fares. Ramadan is the most crowded and most expensive time, and Umrah visas are not issued around Hajj, so avoid planning travel in the Hajj window. Remember the 1448 season deadline: every Umrah visitor must leave Saudi Arabia by 7 April 2027.

Before you fly: the Nusuk app: Download the official Nusuk app and create your account before you travel — our Nusuk app guide walks through setup step by step. Use it to book your Umrah permit and your Rawdah prayer slot in Madinah. Permits are free and are issued only through the official Nusuk app. Book your Rawdah slot as early as you can, because slots fill quickly, and keep your passport and visa details handy when you register.

Practical tips:

  • Wear your Ihram before crossing the Miqat if you are flying directly to Jeddah; the pilot usually announces the Miqat about 45 minutes before landing.
  • Carry some Saudi riyals in cash for small expenses. Cards are widely accepted in Makkah and Madinah, but small shops and taxis may prefer cash.
  • Pack using our Umrah packing list so nothing essential is left behind.
  • Keep your hotel booking reference, return ticket and vaccination certificate in your hand luggage, not in checked bags.
  • Save your agent's number and your hotel address on your phone before you leave India.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Can Indians apply for a Saudi eVisa directly? No. India is not on the tourist eVisa country list. You can only use the eVisa route if you hold a used US, UK or Schengen visa, hold permanent residence in the US, UK or EU, or live in a GCC country. Everyone else uses the Umrah visa through an approved agent.
  • How long is the Umrah visa valid? You must enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the visa being issued, and you can then stay up to 90 days. For the 1448 season, visas stop being issued on 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and all visitors must leave by 7 April 2027.
  • Can I perform Umrah on a stopover in Saudi Arabia? Yes. The free 96-hour stopover visa on Saudia or flynas lets you perform Umrah if you book your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app. It is a short window, so it suits a brief visit rather than a full Umrah trip.
