Umrah from Indonesia — Visa & Flights Guide

Indonesia has the world's largest Muslim population and is Saudi Arabia's biggest Umrah market — well over a million Indonesian pilgrims travel in a normal year. If you are planning Umrah from Indonesia, the visa is the first thing to get right: Indonesia is not among the nationalities eligible for the Saudi tourist eVisa, and there is no visa on arrival on an Indonesian passport. For most Indonesians the visa comes through a licensed PPIU agent registered with Kemenag, although since August 2025 you can also apply directly on the Nusuk Umrah platform. This guide covers both routes, the one-year multiple-entry visa introduced in July 2026, the documents and vaccinations you need, and flights and package prices from Jakarta. Indonesian pilgrims travel under the same broad system as our readers elsewhere in Asia — compare Umrah from Pakistan, Umrah from Bangladesh and Umrah from India, and our original Umrah from Nigeria guide, to see how the routes differ by country.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Indonesians need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Every Indonesian passport holder needs a valid Saudi visa before travelling. Indonesia is not on the tourist eVisa list and there is no visa on arrival for Indonesian citizens travelling on an ordinary Indonesian passport alone (VisaEntryRules; UmrahDIY; Wego's 2026 Umrah visa guide). Anyone who offers you a tourist eVisa on an Indonesian passport alone, without the exception in Route 1 below, is mistaken.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa exception (only if you qualify): An Indonesian passport holder can still apply for the Saudi tourist eVisa if you hold a valid, previously used US, UK or Schengen visa, or you are a legal resident of a GCC country, the EU, the UK or the US. The eVisa costs about SAR 535 and is processed online in minutes to 24 hours (UmrahDIY). Umrah may be performed on it outside the Hajj season, with an Umrah permit booked in the Nusuk app. Our Umrah Visa for UK & US Citizens guide walks through the eVisa steps, and our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out the wider fee picture.

Route 2: the Umrah visa through a PPIU agent (the route most Indonesians use): The standard route is a dedicated Umrah visa arranged by a PPIU — a licensed Umrah travel agent (Penyelenggara Perjalanan Ibadah Umrah) registered with Kemenag, Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs. Only PPIU agents can legally process Umrah visas for Indonesians, so verify your agent's Kemenag registration before paying anything — unregistered sellers are the single biggest source of Umrah fraud in Indonesia. The agent files your application together with verified hotel and transport bookings, because an Umrah visa is no longer issued without them.

Applying directly on Nusuk: Since August 2025, pilgrims can also apply directly on the Nusuk Umrah platform (umrah.nusuk.sa), booking the visa together with verified hotel and transport, instead of going through an agent. Whichever route you use, you still book your Umrah permit and your Rawdah permit yourself in the Nusuk app — our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup step by step.

The one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa (since July 2026): Since July 2026 the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah has issued a one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa: it is valid for 365 days from the date of issue, with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days shared across all your trips in that year. You must obtain an Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before each arrival, and the visa is inactive during the Hajj season — from 1 Dhu al-Qi'dah to 13 Dhu al-Hijjah. For frequent travellers it removes the need to apply again for every trip, but the 90 days do not reset on each entry.

Single-entry rules and the 1448 AH season:

  • A single-entry Umrah visa must now be used within 30 days of issuance — reduced from 90 days — and then allows a stay of up to 90 days (Wego, 2026). Fix your flight dates first, then apply.
  • The 1448 AH season opened on 31 May 2026, with entry to Makkah from 1 June 2026.
  • The last day for Umrah visa issuance is 9 March 2027 (Wego), and as across this site's guides every Umrah visitor must depart before the Hajj season closes the Haram to Umrah pilgrims.
  • One clarification that causes real confusion: since February 2025 Indonesia has been one of 14 nationalities limited to single-entry visit visas — but Umrah and Hajj visas were not part of that restriction, so it does not limit your Umrah options.

Documents and vaccinations checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date.
  • Meningococcal ACWY vaccination, taken at least 10 days before arrival — mandatory for every pilgrim, without exception. Carry the certificate in your hand luggage.
  • Polio vaccination where required under the current Saudi health rules — your PPIU agent will confirm whether it applies to your departure.
  • Your verified hotel and transport bookings, arranged by your PPIU agent or booked by you on the direct Nusuk route, plus a confirmed return ticket.

Flights from Jakarta to Jeddah: Jeddah (King Abdulaziz International, JED) is the usual arrival airport for Makkah. Garuda Indonesia and Saudia fly direct from Jakarta (CGK) to Jeddah, a flight of about 9 hours 55 minutes. Fares checked in November 2026 on Airpaz (airpaz.com): Garuda one-way from about US$499 and Saudia one-way from about US$521; Trip.com lists Garuda Jakarta–Jeddah from Rp 9,625,400 one-way. One-stop options via Emirates (Dubai), Qatar Airways (Doha) and Turkish Airlines (Istanbul) compete on price outside peak weeks. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options; if your package splits the stay between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi; and for where to stay, see Makkah hotels near the Haram and Madinah hotels near Masjid Nabawi.

Packages and paying in instalments: Most Indonesian pilgrims buy a full package from their PPIU agent — flights, visa, hotels, transport and ziyarat in one price. Umrah packages from Jakarta start from about Rp 20 juta per person (yuusr.com, checked 17–18 November 2026), and instalment (cicilan) plans are commonly offered, letting you spread the cost over the months before departure. Whichever package you choose, get the hotel names and their distance from the Haram in writing, and pack with our Umrah packing list. For timing, the best time to perform Umrah guide compares weather, crowds and cost across the year, and our Umrah in Ramadan 2027 guide covers the peak season's dates and deadlines.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Do Indonesians need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Indonesia is not among the nationalities eligible for the Saudi tourist eVisa and there is no visa on arrival on an Indonesian passport. Most Indonesians travel on a dedicated Umrah visa through a PPIU agent licensed by Kemenag, or apply directly on the Nusuk Umrah platform. The only eVisa exception is for holders of a valid, previously used US, UK or Schengen visa, or legal residents of a GCC country, the EU, the UK or the US.
  • Can Indonesians apply on Nusuk directly? Yes. Since August 2025 you can apply on the Nusuk Umrah platform (umrah.nusuk.sa) with verified hotel and transport bookings, without going through an agent. You still need the Nusuk app for your Umrah permit and Rawdah permit once the visa is issued.
  • How long can I stay? On a single-entry Umrah visa, up to 90 days after entry — but you must enter within 30 days of the visa being issued. On the one-year multiple-entry visa introduced in July 2026, you may come and go for 365 days with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days in total, and the visa pauses during the Hajj season.
