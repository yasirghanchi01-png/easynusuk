Umrah from Kenya — Visa & Flights Guide

Travel warning — updated 18 November 2026, read this first: As of 18 November 2026, Saudi Arabia has suspended all visa issuance for travellers arriving from Kenya. The suspension was announced on 30 October 2026 in response to the Ebola outbreak (Bundibugyo strain), after Kenya confirmed its first imported case on 6 October 2026. It covers Umrah, business, family visit and transit visas, and it also bars entry to anyone who was physically in Kenya within 21 days before arrival — even if they travel via a third country. There is no stated end date as of early November 2026: Kenya's government was pushing for the suspension to be lifted on 3 November 2026, and Africa CDC backed a review of the decision. Until it is lifted: do not pay for non-refundable bookings, and confirm the current status with a licensed agent and the Saudi embassy before paying anything at all.

Because of that warning, this guide is written in two parts. Everything below describes the normal route for Umrah from Kenya — the visa options, documents, vaccinations and flights that apply when travel is open — so that you can prepare calmly and book quickly once the suspension ends. Do not treat any of it as a reason to pay a deposit today. When the route reopens, Kenyan pilgrims will travel the same way our readers from other non-eVisa countries do: compare this guide with Umrah from Nigeria and Umrah from Pakistan to see how the agent-based system works across countries.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Kenyans need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Every Kenyan passport holder needs a valid Saudi visa before travelling, and Kenya is not on Saudi Arabia's tourist eVisa list — that position was checked on 3 October 2026 against official sources (Darbiya). There is no visa-free entry and no visa on arrival on a Kenyan passport alone. Anyone who offers you a tourist eVisa on a Kenyan passport alone, without one of the exceptions below, is mistaken.

The normal route: Umrah visa through a licensed agent via Nusuk: When issuance is open, the standard route is a dedicated Umrah visa arranged by a licensed travel agent. The agent files your application through the Nusuk system together with verified hotel and ground transport bookings — since 2025 an Umrah visa cannot be issued without those bookings attached, which is why genuine agents sell packages rather than bare visas. Once you hold the visa, you still book your Umrah permit and your Rawdah permit yourself in the Nusuk app; our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup step by step.

The eVisa exception (only if you qualify): A Kenyan passport holder can still reach the Saudi tourist eVisa through an exception: if you hold a valid US, UK or Schengen visa that you have already used to enter that country or region, you hold permanent residence in the US, UK or an EU country, or you are a GCC resident, you may apply for the visitor eVisa regardless of nationality — and Umrah may be performed on it outside the Hajj season, with a Nusuk permit. Our Umrah Visa for UK & US Citizens guide explains the eVisa steps in detail. Note that while the Kenya suspension is in force, it covers travellers arriving from Kenya however they are documented — the exception does not override it.

The stopover option: If you are flying with Saudia or flynas and transiting through Saudi Arabia, the free 96-hour stopover visa lets you perform Umrah within the stopover window, provided you book your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app. It suits a short visit bolted onto another journey; it is not a substitute for a full Umrah trip, and it is equally unavailable while arrivals from Kenya are suspended.

Key dates for the 1448 AH Umrah season:

  • The last day for Umrah visa issuance is 9 March 2027.
  • The last day to enter Saudi Arabia on an Umrah visa is 23 March 2027.
  • Every Umrah visitor must depart by 7 April 2027.
  • As across the site's other guides, a standard Umrah visa must be used for entry within 30 days of issuance, and the maximum stay after entry is 90 days — so fix your flight dates first, then apply. See our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide for the fee picture.
  • Umrah visas are not issued, and Umrah is not performed, during the Hajj season itself.

Women travelling without a mahram: Women of all ages may now perform Umrah without a mahram, so Kenyan women can travel alone or in a group of women on the normal route. Our guide for women travelling alone covers the current rules and practical safety advice.

Documents checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months beyond your arrival date.
  • Recent passport-size photographs, as your agent specifies.
  • Confirmed return ticket.
  • Proof of accommodation — your verified Nusuk hotel booking, arranged by your agent as part of the package.
  • Some agents also ask for a bank statement — around USD 2,500, per TurKenya (turkenya.com). Treat this as an agent-level requirement rather than a published Saudi rule, and ask your own agent exactly what they need before you pay.

Vaccinations — two certificates matter from Kenya:

  • Meningococcal ACWY — quadrivalent vaccination is mandatory for every Umrah pilgrim, and it must be taken at least 10 days before arrival. Carry the certificate in your hand luggage.
  • Yellow fever — Saudi Arabia lists Kenya as a yellow-fever risk country, so a valid yellow fever vaccination certificate is required. Expect to present it on arrival; do not pack it in a checked bag.

Flights from Nairobi to Jeddah: Jeddah (King Abdulaziz International, JED) is the arrival airport for Makkah. Saudia and Kenya Airways both fly direct from Nairobi (NBO) to Jeddah, a flight of about 3 hours 50 minutes. Fares checked in November 2026 on Airpaz (airpaz.com): Saudia one-way from about US$241–333, and Kenya Airways one-way from about US$555. Average return fares are reported at KSh 56,000–92,000 (flightticketagent.com, January 2026), and connecting budget options on carriers such as Air Arabia and flyadeal start from about KSh 48,000 return. Fares move sharply with the pilgrimage calendar, so treat these as planning figures and check live prices for your dates once travel reopens. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the taxi, bus and train options; for where to stay, see Makkah hotels near the Haram and Madinah hotels near Masjid Nabawi; and pack with our Umrah packing list so nothing essential is left behind.

Best time to go from Kenya: The most comfortable months are October to March, when Makkah and Madinah are at their coolest and fares outside the school holidays are at their kindest. Ramadan is peak season — the most rewarding and the most crowded and expensive time of year; our Umrah in Ramadan 2027 guide gives the expected dates and deadlines, and the best time to perform Umrah guide compares weather, crowds and cost across the whole year. All of this planning, of course, only becomes a booking once the suspension is lifted — prepare now, pay later.
