Umrah from Kosovo — Visa & Flights Guide

Kosovo passport holders can now apply for a Saudi tourist eVisa online. Saudi Arabia added Kosovo (with Guyana) to the eVisa eligibility list on 4 October 2026, and Kosovo appears on the official Permitted Country list in the Visit Saudi eVisa Terms and Conditions (visa.visitsaudi.com). That makes Umrah from Pristina far simpler than before — no embassy visit needed for most travellers.

Visa options: Tourist eVisa — apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com. Fee SAR 535 (about US$143), including health insurance. One-year multiple-entry visa, up to 90 days per visit. Your passport must have at least 6 months' validity from your date of entry. The same visa is also available on arrival at Saudi airports on the same terms. Dedicated Umrah visa — if you prefer a package, apply through Nusuk or a licensed Umrah agent; the agent arranges the visa with hotel and transport bookings documented through Nusuk Masar. 1448H season dates: Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, last entry into Saudi Arabia 23 March 2027, and all pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027. Plan travel inside these dates.

Flights from Pristina: There is currently no direct flight from Pristina (PRN, Adem Jashari International Airport) to Jeddah. The usual routes are Turkish Airlines via Istanbul (PRN–IST about 2 hours, IST–JED 3 hours 45 minutes) — around 6–7 hours in total with a connection; Pegasus Airlines via Istanbul Sabiha Gokcen (SAW) — the budget alternative on the same corridor; Qatar Airways via Doha — another one-stop option. Book early for Ramadan and school-holiday dates, when one-stop seats fill first.

Money and time: Kosovo uses the euro (EUR). Pristina is 2 hours behind Jeddah in winter (CET, UTC+1 vs AST, UTC+3) and 1 hour behind in summer (CEST, UTC+2). Keep the difference in mind when booking onward transport and hotel check-in times.

Health requirements: The meningococcal ACWY vaccine is mandatory for Umrah travellers and must be given at least 10 days before arrival. Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Kosovo, and yellow fever vaccination is only required if you arrive from a yellow-fever risk country. Carry your vaccination certificate with your travel documents.

Women travelling: Women of all ages from Kosovo can travel for Umrah without a mahram, under the current Saudi rules — the same rule that applies to other eVisa-eligible nationalities.

A reminder of the reward: The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: 'Umrah to Umrah expiates what is between them' (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773, Sahih Muslim 1349).
