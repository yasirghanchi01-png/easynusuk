Umrah from Lithuania — Visa & Flights Guide

Lithuanian pilgrims have one of the easiest Umrah routes in Europe: Lithuania is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, so you can apply for a tourist eVisa online before you fly, get a visa on arrival, or still use the dedicated Umrah visa route when you prefer a package. Lithuania is an EU member and a Schengen member. There are no direct flights from Vilnius to Jeddah, so every route to Jeddah is one stop — the smoothest runs via Istanbul with Turkish Airlines. This guide covers both visa routes, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules, and the flights and fares. Travelling from elsewhere in Europe? Our newest companion guide, Umrah from Latvia, covers the same eVisa route for travellers from Latvia, and our Umrah from Slovenia and Umrah from Estonia guides cover it for travellers from Slovenia and Estonia.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Umrah to Umrah expiates what is between them." (Sahih al-Bukhari 1773, Sahih Muslim 1349)

Do Lithuanian pilgrims need a visa for Umrah? Yes — but you can get yours online in advance or at the airport. Lithuania is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, and the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk remains available when you prefer everything arranged in a package. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out how the fees compare across the visa types.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa (or visa on arrival): Apply online at the official portal visa.visitsaudi.com. The eVisa costs SAR 535 in total, including the mandatory medical insurance (about US$143, about €125). It is valid for 1 year with multiple entries and allows up to 90 days per visit. Your passport must be valid for at least six months from entry, and most applications are approved within minutes to 24 hours. Umrah is allowed on it outside the Hajj season — book the Umrah permit on the Nusuk app — and it is not valid for Hajj or work. A visa on arrival is available on the same terms for Lithuanian passport holders at Saudi international airports.

Route 2: the Umrah visa via Nusuk or an agent: Apply through the Nusuk app or a licensed travel agent, and enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the visa being issued. Since 20 July 2026 there is also a multiple-entry Umrah visa: valid for 365 days with up to 90 days of cumulative stay — each visit needs its own Nusuk service package and Umrah permit — and it is not usable during the Hajj season. Our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup and the permit booking step by step.

The 1448H season dates:

  • Umrah visas for the 1448H season have been issued since 31 May 2026.
  • Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • All Umrah visa holders must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • Meningococcal ACWY vaccination is mandatory, at least 10 days before arrival — the conjugate vaccine is valid for 5 years and the polysaccharide vaccine for 3 years — with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Lithuania.
  • A yellow fever certificate is needed only if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram — our Umrah for women travelling alone guide has the rules and practical detail.

Flights from Lithuania to Jeddah: There are no direct flights from Vilnius (VNO) to Jeddah (JED). The best route is Turkish Airlines via Istanbul: Vilnius–Istanbul is nonstop at about 2h 50m, and Istanbul–Jeddah takes about 3h 45m, for a total journey of about 8h to 9h including the layover. Return fares from Vilnius to Saudi Arabia run from about €701 with Brussels Airlines (Vilnius–Riyadh) to about €732 with Lufthansa/SWISS (Vilnius–Riyadh), and Jeddah via Istanbul is in a similar range — book early and compare on the airline sites. One-stop alternatives run via Frankfurt (Lufthansa), Warsaw (LOT, including flight LO-798 Vilnius–Jeddah), Doha (Qatar Airways) or Vienna (Austrian). Fly into Jeddah (JED) for Makkah; Madinah (MED) is the other gateway. Fares are indicative, checked 23 Nov 2026, and change daily — check live prices before booking. Lithuania uses the euro (EUR), and cards are widely accepted in Saudi Arabia. Vilnius is 1 hour behind Jeddah in winter and on the same time as Jeddah in summer, while Jeddah stays on AST (UTC+3) all year — once you land, set your prayer alarms by the local Makkah and Madinah times in this site's Namaz section. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and if your stay splits between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi, while our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: On the eVisa route you pay the SAR 535 visa fee yourself and book flights and hotels separately; on the Umrah visa route the visa comes inside the package. Flights are the biggest variable, then hotels near the Haram. See our Makkah hotels guide before you book, and add up the full budget — visa, flights, hotels, food and local transport — before you commit.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — tourist eVisa online or on arrival, or the Umrah visa via Nusuk or a licensed Lithuanian travel agent.
  3. Get the meningococcal ACWY vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your flights and hotels (or your package on the Umrah visa route).
  5. Book the free Umrah permit on the Nusuk app before entering Masjid al-Haram.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

A short practical checklist: Book the Rawdah permit in the Nusuk app as soon as your visa is approved, because slots go quickly, and keep your eVisa and passport together at Jeddah airport; keep your passport valid for at least six months and carry a printed copy of your approved eVisa with it; install the Nusuk app and book your Umrah permit before you fly; choose a hotel near the Haram in Makkah so the five daily prayers are an easy walk — our Makkah hotels guide compares the areas; pack your Ihram garments on top of your bag and read our Ihram rules guide so you are ready to enter Ihram before the miqat announcement on board; and work through our Umrah packing list so nothing essential is left behind.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. Choose your dates with the best time to perform Umrah guide, which compares weather, crowds and cost across the year, and learn the supplications from Tawaf Duas and the wider Umrah Duas collection before you fly, so the rites themselves feel familiar when you reach the Haram.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Is Lithuania on the Saudi tourist eVisa list? Yes — Lithuania is on the official list in the eVisa Terms and Conditions at visa.visitsaudi.com (checked 23 November 2026). Lithuanian citizens apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com.
  • Can I perform Umrah on the tourist eVisa? Yes, outside the Hajj season, with the Umrah permit booked on Nusuk. The eVisa costs SAR 535 including mandatory health insurance (about US$143, about €125), lasts 1 year with multiple entries, and allows up to 90 days per visit; a visa on arrival is available on the same terms for Lithuanian passport holders. It is not valid for Hajj or work.
  • Are there direct flights from Lithuania to Jeddah? No — there is no direct Vilnius to Jeddah flight. The best route is Turkish Airlines via Istanbul: Vilnius to Istanbul is nonstop at about 2h 50m, and Istanbul to Jeddah takes about 3h 45m, roughly 8h to 9h in total including the layover. Alternatives run via Frankfurt with Lufthansa, or via Warsaw, Doha or Vienna.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and all Umrah visa holders must depart by 7 April 2027.
