Umrah from Malaysia — Visa & Flights Guide

Malaysia is one of the easiest passports for Umrah: it is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, so Malaysians can apply online in minutes or get a visa on arrival, and still use the dedicated Umrah visa route when they prefer a package. This guide covers both routes, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules — including Makkah Route pre-clearance at KLIA — and Kuala Lumpur flights and fares. Compare the eVisa route here with Umrah from Turkey, contrast it with the agent-based routes in Umrah from Egypt, Umrah from Indonesia and our newest guide, Umrah from Morocco, and see how the picture looks in Umrah from Pakistan.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Malaysians need a visa for Umrah? Yes — but unlike most of our readers, you can get yours online in minutes or at the airport. Malaysia is on the Saudi tourist eVisa list, and the dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk remains available when you prefer everything arranged in a package. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out how the fees compare across the visa types.

Route 1: the tourist eVisa: Apply at visa.visitsaudi.com. The fee is SAR 535, including health cover up to SAR 100,000. The visa is valid for one year with multiple entries and a cumulative stay of up to 90 days, and it covers Umrah outside the Hajj season — never Hajj. A visa on arrival on the same terms is available at Saudi international airports.

Route 2: the Umrah visa via Nusuk: Buy a service package from an approved provider on the Nusuk platform — direct application has been possible since August 2025 — or go through a licensed Malaysian agency registered with MOTAC, or a Tabung Haji package. A standard Umrah visa must be used to enter within 30 days of issue, then allows a stay of up to 90 days. Get your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before arrival — our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup step by step.

Route 3: the one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa (since July 2026): Since July 2026 Saudi Arabia issues a one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa: it is valid for 365 days, with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days. Each visit needs a service package from an approved Nusuk provider and an Umrah permit before arrival, and the visa is inactive during the Hajj season.

The 1448H season dates:

  • The Umrah season opened 31 May 2026.
  • Umrah visas may be issued until 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • Pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • ACWY meningococcal vaccination at least 10 days before arrival, with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Malaysia.
  • Yellow fever vaccination is only needed if you are arriving from, or transiting, a risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram.
  • Malaysia is covered by the Makkah Route initiative, so on selected flights immigration pre-clearance can be completed at KLIA before departure — check with your airline whether your flight is a Makkah Route flight.

Flights from Kuala Lumpur to Jeddah: Kuala Lumpur (KUL) to Jeddah (JED) direct takes about 9 hours 10 minutes to 9 hours 40 minutes, flown by Malaysia Airlines, Saudia and AirAsia X; Malaysia Airlines and Saudia also fly Kuala Lumpur to Madinah (MED) direct. One-way fares start from about US$266 on AirAsia X, and from about US$540 on full-service carriers (Malaysia Airlines, Saudia); round trips start from about US$600. All fares checked 19 Nov 2026, prices change daily. From Penang, Kota Kinabalu or Kuching, connect through Kuala Lumpur. Jeddah is 5 hours behind Kuala Lumpur. Book the Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before you fly. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: Roughly, in ringgit: the tourist eVisa is about RM620 (SAR 535). Flights are the biggest variable, then hotels near the Haram. See our general Umrah cost guide and the Makkah hotels guide before you book.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — eVisa online or on arrival for a DIY trip, or an Umrah visa package via Nusuk or a licensed agency.
  3. Get the ACWY vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your service package (if on the Umrah visa) and flights.
  5. Get your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. Learn the supplications from Umrah Duas and Tawaf Duas before you fly, so the rites themselves feel familiar when you reach the Haram.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Can Malaysians get a Saudi tourist eVisa? Yes — Malaysia is on the tourist eVisa list. Apply at visa.visitsaudi.com for SAR 535 including health cover up to SAR 100,000; the visa is valid one year with multiple entries and a cumulative stay of up to 90 days, and a visa on arrival on the same terms is available at Saudi international airports.
  • Can I perform Umrah on the tourist eVisa? Yes, outside the Hajj season — it never covers Hajj — and you still need your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before arrival.
  • What is the Makkah Route? Malaysia is covered by the Makkah Route initiative, so on selected flights immigration pre-clearance can be completed at KLIA before departure. Check with your airline whether your flight is a Makkah Route flight.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.
