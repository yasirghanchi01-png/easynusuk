Umrah from Morocco — Visa & Flights Guide

Moroccan pilgrims cannot use the Saudi tourist eVisa — Morocco is not on the eVisa list — so the Umrah visa through Nusuk or a licensed agent is the normal route. This guide covers the visa options, the 1448H season dates, the documents and health rules, and direct flights from Casablanca. Moroccan pilgrims travel under the same broad system as readers of our other non-eVisa country guides — compare Umrah from Egypt and Umrah from Kenya, contrast it with Umrah from Turkey, where the tourist eVisa does exist, and see our Asian guide to Umrah from Pakistan.

"And [due] to Allah from the people is a pilgrimage to the House — for whoever is able to find thereto a way." (Quran 3:97, Sahih International)

Do Moroccans need a visa for Umrah? Yes. Every Moroccan passport holder needs a valid Saudi visa before travelling, and Morocco is not among the nationalities eligible for the Saudi tourist eVisa, so for almost every Moroccan pilgrim the visa is a dedicated Umrah visa arranged through the Nusuk platform or a licensed Moroccan Umrah agent. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide sets out the wider fee picture across the visa types.

Route 1: the Umrah visa via Nusuk: Apply on the Nusuk Umrah platform — direct application has been possible since August 2025 — or through a licensed Moroccan Umrah agent. A service package (hotel + transport) from an approved Nusuk provider is required, and you must get your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before arrival. A standard Umrah visa must be used to enter within 30 days of issue, and then allows a stay of up to 90 days. Our Nusuk app guide walks through the setup and the permit booking step by step.

Route 2: the one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa (since July 2026): Since July 2026 Saudi Arabia issues a one-year multiple-entry Umrah visa: it is valid for 365 days, with a cumulative stay of up to 90 days. Each visit needs a service package from an approved Nusuk provider and an Umrah permit before arrival, and the visa is inactive during the Hajj season.

Route 3: the exception — a used Western visa or residence: Moroccans holding a used US, UK or Schengen tourist or business visa, or a permanent residence permit from the US, the UK or an EU country, can apply for the tourist eVisa through the KSA Visa portal or get a visa on arrival. Moroccans living in France with a valid French residence permit are routed to the online visa on the KSA Visa portal.

A route that does not exist — the Package Visa: Morocco is not on the Package Visa list — the Ministry of Tourism names only seven countries (Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico, Pakistan) — so do not plan around it.

The 1448H season dates:

  • The Umrah season opened 31 May 2026.
  • Umrah visas may be issued until 9 March 2027.
  • The last entry date is 23 March 2027.
  • Pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.

Documents and health checklist:

  • Passport valid for at least six months at entry.
  • ACWY meningococcal vaccination at least 10 days before arrival, with the certificate ready to show.
  • Polio vaccination is not compulsory for travellers from Morocco.
  • Yellow fever vaccination is only needed if you are arriving from, or transiting, a yellow-fever risk country.
  • Women of all ages may travel for Umrah without a mahram.
  • Children need their own passport and visa.

Flights from Casablanca to Jeddah: Casablanca Mohammed V (CMN) to Jeddah (JED) direct takes about 6 hours, flown by Saudia (about 14 times a week), Royal Air Maroc (about 4 times a week) and flynas; Saudia also flies Casablanca to Madinah (MED) direct. One-way fares start from about US$309 (Saudia, November 2026); round trips start from about US$460, typically about US$650–880. All fares checked 19 Nov 2026, prices change daily. From Marrakech, Tangier, Agadir or Fes, connect through Casablanca, or through Istanbul or Doha. Jeddah is 2 hours ahead of Casablanca (3 hours during Ramadan, when Morocco's clocks go back an hour). Book the Umrah permit in the Nusuk app before you fly, and arrive at Jeddah with your hotel and transport already booked. On arrival, our Jeddah Airport to Makkah guide covers the onward taxi, bus and train options, and if your stay splits between the two holy cities, the Makkah to Madinah transport guide compares the Haramain train, bus and taxi, while our Madinah hotels guide helps you choose where to stay near the Prophet's Mosque.

What it costs: The visa comes inside the Umrah package, so compare packages from licensed agents rather than paying for a visa alone. Flights are the biggest variable, then hotels near the Haram. See our general Umrah cost guide and the Makkah hotels guide before you book.

Step by step:

  1. Choose your dates inside the 1448H season.
  2. Pick the visa route — Umrah visa via Nusuk or an agent, or the eVisa exception if you hold a used US/UK/Schengen visa or Western residence.
  3. Get the ACWY vaccine at least 10 days before travel.
  4. Book your service package and flights.
  5. Get your Umrah permit in the Nusuk app.
  6. Book the Rawdah permit too if you will visit Madinah.
  7. Pack using our Umrah packing list.

Preparing for the journey itself: With the visa and flights settled, the rest is preparation. Learn the supplications from Umrah Duas and Tawaf Duas before you fly, so the rites themselves feel familiar when you reach the Haram.

Frequently asked questions:

  • Can Moroccans get a Saudi tourist eVisa? Not on a Moroccan passport alone — Morocco is not on the eVisa list. The only exception is for Moroccans holding a used US, UK or Schengen tourist or business visa, or a permanent residence permit from the US, the UK or an EU country, who can apply through the KSA Visa portal or get a visa on arrival.
  • How do most Moroccans get their Umrah visa? Through a dedicated Umrah visa arranged on the Nusuk Umrah platform or by a licensed Moroccan Umrah agent, with a service package (hotel + transport) from an approved Nusuk provider, and the Umrah permit booked in the Nusuk app before arrival.
  • Is Morocco on the Package Visa list? No. The Ministry of Tourism names only seven countries for the Package Visa — Jordan, Egypt, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Mexico and Pakistan.
  • When does the Umrah season end in 1448H? Visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027, and pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027.
