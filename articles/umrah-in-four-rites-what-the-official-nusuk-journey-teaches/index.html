Umrah in Four Rites: What the Official Nusuk Journey Teaches

The official Nusuk Umrah Journey of the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah describes Umrah as four essential rites, in this order: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i, and shaving or trimming the hair. Allah says: "And complete the Hajj and Umrah for Allah…" (Qur'an 2:196).

  • Ihram begins with a sincere intention and the Talbiyah, at or before the Meeqat — the journey starts in the heart before it starts on the road.
  • Tawaf: enter the Mataf, stand in line with the Black Stone on your left, raise the hand and say "Allahu Akbar", and circle the Kaaba seven times anti-clockwise, filling the rounds with dua and dhikr.
  • Sa'i: following the signs inside Masjid al-Haram to Safa, walk between Safa and Marwah seven lengths, beginning at Safa.
  • Hair: shaving or trimming completes the Umrah — what Ihram had restricted becomes permitted again.
