Umrah from Pakistan — Complete Guide 2026

Every year, hundreds of thousands of Pakistanis travel for Umrah — but the Pakistani route has its own rules, and the first one surprises many people: Pakistani passport holders cannot apply for an Umrah visa directly on Nusuk. Your application must go through a licensed Umrah tour operator attested by Pakistan's Ministry of Religious Affairs, working with a Saudi-approved service provider. Anyone offering you a "direct" or "visa-only, no operator" route is not following the system. This guide walks through the whole journey — visa, costs, flights and permits — and if it is your first time, read it alongside Umrah for First-Timers.

The hotel booking rule (Nusuk Masar) — read this first: Since 10 June 2025, under the Nusuk Masar rule, a confirmed hotel booking with a Booking Reference Number (BRN) is mandatory before any visa is issued. Visa-only applications are not accepted — your operator books the hotels through the Masar system first, then the visa follows. This is why honest operators quote packages, not bare visas.

What it costs (2026):

  • Saudi government visa fee: SAR 300 — about PKR 22,300 at recent rates.
  • All-in visa cost from Pakistan: typically PKR 48,000–65,000, including the mandatory medical insurance and the operator's processing.
  • Full Umrah packages: generally PKR 220,000–500,000+, depending on the season and the hotel tier — distance from the Haram is the biggest price driver. Our Umrah Visa Fees 2026 guide breaks the costs down further, and Umrah Visa 2026 covers the visa types.
  • A warning on numbers: the SAR–PKR rate moves daily, so every rupee figure here is a guide, not a promise — confirm the exact amount with your operator before you pay.

Documents and timing:

  • Passport valid at least 6 months from your date of entry — check it before you book anything.
  • Entry window: 30 days from issuance — you must enter Saudi Arabia within 30 days of the visa being issued (this changed in October 2025), so do not apply too early.
  • The Hajj pause: Umrah visas pause about 10–15 days before Hajj and resume after Hajj — plan around it.
  • The 1448 AH season opened with visas from 31 May 2026 and entry from 1 June 2026.

Vaccinations:

  • Polio (OPV) is required for Pakistani travellers — get the drops and keep the record.
  • Meningococcal ACWY: Saudi Arabia suspended the mandatory requirement in February 2025, but many operators and health guidance still list it. Check the current rule with your operator, and if you have the certificate, carry it.
  • Your certificate, digitally: the NADRA digital vaccination certificate is available through the NIMS portal for PKR 100 — carry a printout as well as the digital copy.

Flights: Direct and one-stop flights run from Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad to Jeddah or Madinah — PIA, Saudia and Airblue, plus the Gulf carriers. Flying into Madinah and out of Jeddah (or the reverse) saves a long road transfer. Book early — fares climb steeply in the peak months.

After you arrive: Your visa came through an operator, but your permits are your own job: use the Nusuk app for your Umrah permit and your Rawdah visit slot — our Nusuk App Guide and Rawdah Permit guides walk through both, step by step. Women can travel for Umrah without a mahram under current Saudi rules — see Women Travelling Alone for Umrah for the details and safety advice. For money, carry Saudi riyals in cash plus your bank cards; for luggage, the Umrah Packing List covers everything, including the documents folder.

Best time to go: November to February for the coolest weather and the easiest Tawaf. Ramadan carries unmatched reward — but it is the busiest and priciest season of the year, so book it many months ahead.

Quick questions:

  • Can I apply for an Umrah visa myself on Nusuk from Pakistan? No. Pakistani passport holders cannot apply directly on Nusuk — your application must go through a licensed Umrah tour operator attested by Pakistan's Ministry of Religious Affairs, working with a Saudi-approved service provider. Since 10 June 2025 your operator must also hold a confirmed hotel booking (BRN) on Nusuk Masar before the visa can be issued.
  • How much does Umrah from Pakistan cost in 2026? The Saudi government visa fee is SAR 300 (about PKR 22,300); the all-in visa cost from Pakistan is typically PKR 48,000–65,000 with mandatory medical insurance and processing; full packages generally run PKR 220,000–500,000+ by season and hotel tier. The SAR–PKR rate moves daily — confirm the exact figure with your operator.
  • Which vaccinations do Pakistani pilgrims need? Polio (OPV) is required. The meningococcal ACWY requirement was suspended by Saudi Arabia in February 2025, but many operators still list it — check the current rule with your operator and carry your certificate if you have one.
