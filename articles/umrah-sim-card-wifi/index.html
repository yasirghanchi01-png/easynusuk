Staying Connected on Umrah: SIM Cards, Data and WiFi in Makkah and Madinah

Why it matters: You need mobile data for the Nusuk app and permits, maps, hotel bookings and staying in touch with family. Buy a Saudi visitor SIM at the airport and you are connected within minutes of landing.

Where to buy: STC, Mobily and Zain all have kiosks in the arrivals hall at Jeddah King Abdulaziz Airport Terminal 1 and at Madinah Airport, plus stores in malls in Makkah and Madinah. Bring your passport and visa. Saudi Arabia requires real-name registration for every mobile line, including prepaid visitor SIMs.

STC (Sawa Visitor, widest coverage in the Kingdom): Visitor 26, SAR 31, 6 GB data, 1 hour calls, 2 weeks. Visitor 47, SAR 55, 21 GB data, 2 hours calls, 2 weeks, the best fit for a typical 10 to 14 day Umrah. Visitor 65, SAR 75, 33 GB data, 3 hours calls, 3 weeks. Visitor 86, SAR 100, 51 GB data, 5 hours calls, 4 weeks.

Mobily (Visitors, often the best value per GB): Visitors 30, SAR 34.50, 5 GB data, 60 minutes, 14 days. Visitors 50, SAR 57.50, 20 GB data, 120 minutes, 14 days. Visitors 65, SAR 74.75, 30 GB data, 200 minutes, 21 days. Visitors 90, SAR 103.50, 55 GB data, 300 minutes, 30 days.

Zain (good value, slightly smaller coverage): Zain sells visitor packages aimed at Hajj and Umrah travellers, but it does not publish a clear visitor price table, so check the prices at the airport kiosk before you buy. Reported airport prices in 2026 were around SAR 120 for 30 GB plus social media data and 1,000 minutes for 28 days.

Prices include 15 percent VAT. Confirm the price at the point of sale, because packages change.

eSIM before you fly: If your phone supports eSIM, you can buy a Saudi travel eSIM before departure and land already connected. Check your phone supports eSIM first, and that the plan covers the whole trip.

WiFi: Most hotels in Makkah and Madinah offer free WiFi, and the airports offer free WiFi too. Do not rely on hotel WiFi alone for Nusuk permits, keep your own data as the backup.

WhatsApp calling: Regular voice and video calls over the internet are restricted in Saudi Arabia, so WhatsApp calls often do not connect. WhatsApp text messages and voice notes work normally. Use the local and international minutes included in your visitor package for calls home.

Save data: Download offline maps of Makkah and Madinah before you travel, and take screenshots of your Nusuk permits in case the signal drops inside the Haram.
