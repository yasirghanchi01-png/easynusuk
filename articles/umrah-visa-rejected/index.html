Umrah Visa Rejected? Common Reasons & How to Reapply

A rejected Umrah visa feels like the end of the journey, but most refusals come down to a small number of fixable issues. Saudi authorities do not publish a complete list of rejection reasons, and there is no standard public appeal process — in practice you find the reason, fix it, and submit a new application. Here are the reasons that come up most often, and what to do about each one.

1. Incorrect or inconsistent details — A wrong date of birth, a mistyped passport number, or a name spelled differently from the passport can sink an application. The official eVisa terms require true and accurate information and say an application with false or misleading details will be rejected. Once a tourist eVisa application is submitted it cannot be cancelled or refunded, so check every field against the passport before you pay.

2. Passport validity too short — The tourist eVisa requires at least 6 months of passport validity from your date of entry into Saudi Arabia. If your passport expires inside that window, renew it first and apply second — this is the cheapest fix of all.

3. Missing or invalid Nusuk Masar booking — Since 10 June 2025 a dedicated Umrah visa depends on accommodation and transport being documented and approved on the Nusuk Masar platform, and since January 2026 pre-booked accommodation through Nusuk-approved hotels and transport through licensed providers is required before the visa application is submitted. A hotel booking that cannot be verified — never real, already cancelled, or made through a channel the system cannot see — will block the visa. Book only through Nusuk-approved hotels and licensed transport providers, and keep the booking references.

4. ACWY vaccine record problems — Saudi Arabia asks every Hajj and Umrah traveller for a meningococcal ACWY vaccination record, with no exemption by nationality. The dose must have been given at least 10 days before arrival and within the last 3 years (polysaccharide vaccine) or 5 years (conjugate vaccine). The certificate must clearly state the vaccine name, its type and the date given — if the type is missing, the record counts as valid for 3 years only. An expired dose, or one given fewer than 10 days before arrival, is rejected even though a record exists.

5. Previous overstay or violation — If you overstayed a Saudi visa before, or were deported, later applications are likely to be refused. An open violation record leads to refusal, and this reason cannot be fixed by reapplying quickly.

6. Wrong channel or an unauthorised agent — There are three legitimate routes to Umrah: a dedicated Umrah visa through the Nusuk Umrah platform, a licensed travel agent, or a tourist eVisa for eligible nationalities. Unlicensed agents cause refusals: in February 2026 the Ministry suspended three Umrah companies over failures to provide approved accommodation, and a separate suspension affected 1,800 overseas agencies. Use only licensed agents and the official portals (visa.visitsaudi.com, Nusuk).

7. Photo problems — Photos must be recent, clear, on a white background, with the face fully visible. A cropped, dark or old photo is a common avoidable refusal.

8. Name mismatch across documents — The name, date of birth and passport number must match exactly across the passport, national ID (CNIC where applicable) and the application. Even a small spelling difference between documents can trigger a refusal.

9. Season cut-off dates — For the 1448H season, Umrah visa issuance ends 9 March 2027, the last entry is 23 March 2027 and all pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027. Applications filed too close to the cut-off may not be processed. A dedicated Umrah visa issued in 2026 is valid for 30 days from issuance, so time your application to your travel dates rather than filing months ahead.

What to do after a rejection, step by step:

  1. Find the exact reason — check the application portal, the rejection email, or your provider's notice. If an agency applied for you, ask it for the specific status or error, not a general answer.
  2. Fix the underlying problem — correct the detail, replace the document, or sort out the booking before you apply again.
  3. Check your passport — renew first if it will have less than 6 months left on arrival.
  4. Pick the right visa type — if you are not eligible for the eVisa, a dedicated Umrah visa through Nusuk or a licensed agent is the route.
  5. Check the fee before reapplying — tourist eVisa fees are non-refundable when an application is unsuccessful, so a new application usually means paying again.
  6. Double-check everything before you submit — ask someone else to read your details against your passport.

Pre-application checklist:

  • Passport valid 6+ months from arrival date
  • Name, date of birth and passport number identical on every document
  • Recent photo, white background, face fully visible
  • ACWY certificate with correct vaccine, type and date, at least 10 days before arrival
  • Hotel and transport booked through Nusuk-approved providers, references saved
  • Application made on the official portal or through a licensed agent
  • Travel dates inside the 1448H season (entry before 23 March 2027, depart by 7 April 2027)
