Umrah Visa - Status Check

How to Check Your Umrah Visa Status Online

After your application goes in, the waiting is the hardest part. The good news is that you can check umrah visa status online yourself, free of charge, on official Saudi portals. You do not need to rely only on your agent's updates. This guide shows what to prepare before you check, the three official places to check, and what each status result means.

What you need before checking: Have these details ready, copied exactly as they appear in your documents: your passport number, exactly as printed; your visa number or application number, or the reference your operator gave you; your first name as printed in the passport; and your nationality. A single wrong character can make a real application look missing, so copy each detail slowly and check it twice.

Place 1 - The Saudi MOFA visa platform (visa.mofa.gov.sa):

  1. Open visa.mofa.gov.sa in your browser.
  2. Choose the enquiry or search option for visa applications.
  3. Enter any two identifiers, for example your passport number together with your visa number or application number.
  4. Choose your nationality, complete the captcha, and press Inquire.
  5. If the visa is approved, the full visa document is shown. Save it as a PDF and print a copy for your travel folder.

Place 2 - The KSA Visa portal (ksavisa.sa):

  1. Open ksavisa.sa and click Track Application.
  2. Enter your visa or application number and your passport number.
  3. Complete the captcha and click Enquiry.
  4. Click View to see the full visa document when it is ready.

Place 3 - Your Nusuk account (umrah.nusuk.sa): If you applied directly through Nusuk, sign in at umrah.nusuk.sa and open My Applications or My Bookings. Your visa status appears there alongside your package and permit details, which makes it the easiest place to follow everything for one trip in a single view. Our Nusuk app guide explains the account sections in more detail.

What the statuses mean: Under process means the application is still being worked on, so wait and check again later. Approved or issued means your visa is ready: open the full document, save the PDF and print it. Rejected means the application was refused. Not found usually means the details you entered do not match a record yet, which is often a typing error or a very recent submission rather than a refusal.

What to do for each result: If an agent submitted your application, wait at least 72 hours before checking, because new applications take time to appear. If nothing appears after that, re-check your numbers character by character against the passport, and ask your operator for the correct application reference if the one you have does not work. If the status says rejected, read our Umrah visa rejection guide before paying a new fee, so you fix the real reason first. If you are still preparing your application, the new booking requirements are explained in our Umrah Visa 2026 Transport Rule article, and the visa types themselves are covered in the Umrah Visa 2026 guide.

One safety warning: Always type the portal addresses yourself into your browser. Never follow a visa link sent in an unexpected message, even if it looks official. Fake visa pages are a common way to steal passport details and payments, and the real portals above cost nothing to use.
