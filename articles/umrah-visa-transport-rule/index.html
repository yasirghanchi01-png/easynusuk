Umrah Visa 2026 Transport Rule - What Changed in January

If you are planning Umrah this year, the umrah visa 2026 transport rule is the change most likely to catch you out. Since January 2026, the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah requires every Umrah visa applicant to arrange accommodation and transport before the visa application is submitted, not after the visa arrives. Hotels and transport are no longer the last step of planning a trip. They are now part of the application itself, and an application that skips them cannot move forward.

What you must book first: Accommodation must be pre-booked through Nusuk-approved hotels, and transport must be pre-booked through licensed Nusuk-compliant providers. The transport booking has to cover the whole journey on the ground: your arrival from the airport, the Makkah to Madinah leg, and your departure. The system checks for valid confirmations for these bookings and blocks progress without them, so an application cannot be completed on the promise that bookings will be made later.

How the bookings join up with the visa: Your hotel and transport bookings are linked to your visa through a Booking Reference Number (BRN). Keep this number safe and share it with anyone helping you with the application, because it is the reference that ties your confirmed bookings to your visa file. If the BRN and the bookings behind it cannot be verified, the application stalls at exactly this point. Our general Umrah Visa 2026 guide explains the visa types this rule sits alongside.

The shorter entry window: A single-entry Umrah visa is now valid for only 30 days from issuance to enter Saudi Arabia, down from 90 days. If the pilgrim has not entered within that window, the visa is automatically cancelled. Once you are inside the Kingdom, the stay allowed is up to 90 days. A multiple-entry Umrah visa works differently: it is valid for one year from issuance, with up to 90 days of stay in total across that year. Because the single-entry window is now so short, timing matters: apply close enough to your travel dates that the 30 days do not run out before you fly.

Season deadlines for the 1448 AH cycle: The last date to issue an Umrah visa is 9 March 2027. The last date for pilgrim entry is 23 March 2027. All pilgrims must depart by 7 April 2027. Applications filed close to these dates leave no room for a failed booking check, so build your bookings well before the deadlines rather than in the final days.

How this rule causes rejections: Most problems under the new rule come from the bookings, not the pilgrim. A hotel reference that cannot be verified, perhaps because it was never a real confirmed booking or was made outside the approved channels, will fail the check. Missing transport confirmations for any leg of the journey, including the return to the airport, leave the application incomplete. A booking made through a provider that is not in good standing can also fail, even when the pilgrim paid in good faith. Remember too that tourist eVisa fees are non-refundable, so a failed application can mean paying again. Our Umrah visa rejection guide walks through what to do next, and Umrah Visa Fees 2026 explains the costs before you pay.

Checklist before you apply:

  • Passport valid for 6 months beyond your entry date
  • Names entered exactly as they appear on the passport
  • Hotel booking made through a Nusuk-approved hotel, with a reference that can be verified
  • Transport confirmations in hand for airport arrival, the Makkah to Madinah leg and departure
  • Transport provider licensed and Nusuk-compliant
  • Booking Reference Number (BRN) saved and ready to enter
