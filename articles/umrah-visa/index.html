Umrah Visa 2026: Types, Requirements and How to Apply

There is more than one way to get a visa that allows Umrah in 2026. Which route applies to you depends on your nationality and the visas you already hold. This guide explains each type, what documents you need, and the health and permit rules that apply to every pilgrim.

1. Tourist eVisa. Citizens of 60+ eligible countries — including the US, UK, EU countries, Canada, Australia and Japan — can apply online at visa.visitsaudi.com. The government fee is around SAR 300 plus mandatory medical insurance. The eVisa is valid for 1 year, multiple entry, with up to 90 days per stay. It allows Umrah outside Hajj season, but it does NOT allow Hajj.

2. Dedicated Umrah visa. For nationalities that are not eVisa-eligible — including Pakistan, India and Bangladesh — the Umrah visa is arranged through a licensed Umrah agent or the Nusuk Umrah platform. It is single entry and typically valid for 30 days. Since June 2025, accommodation must be registered on the Nusuk Masar system through a licensed hotel.

3. Visa on arrival. Holders of a valid US, UK or Schengen visa can obtain a visa on arrival in Saudi Arabia, which can be used to perform Umrah.

4. Other valid Saudi visas. Holders of any valid Saudi visa — including work, family visit, business and transit visas — can also perform Umrah.

Required documents:

  • Passport valid for 6+ months
  • Recent photograph
  • Confirmed accommodation
  • Return ticket
  • Medical insurance

Vaccination. Meningitis (ACWY) vaccination is mandatory — it must be taken at least 10 days before travel, and you must carry an International Certificate of Vaccination as proof.

Women travelling without a mahram. Women aged 18+ can travel for Umrah without a mahram.

Visa is not the permit. The Umrah permit is separate from the visa and is booked free in the Nusuk app — you need both before you enter Masjid al-Haram for Umrah.

Hajj season cut-off. Umrah visa issuance stops weeks before Hajj season each year, so apply well in advance if you plan to travel close to Hajj.

Please note: Fees and rules can change. Always confirm the latest fees, eligibility and requirements on the official portals before applying.
