Umrah Visa Fees 2026 — Cost, Types & How to Apply

Before the flights and the hotels, every Umrah journey starts with one question: what does the visa itself cost? In 2026 there are four realistic routes — the tourist eVisa, visa on arrival, the dedicated Umrah visa and the free stopover visa — and they are priced differently. Which one is yours depends mostly on your passport. The figures below were checked on 16 November 2026.

Please note: Visa fees change often. Treat the amounts here as a guide for planning, and always confirm the final amount on the official portal — visa.visitsaudi.com for the tourist eVisa and umrah.nusuk.sa for the Umrah visa — before you pay.

The tourist eVisa, honestly priced: The eVisa is open to citizens of 60+ eligible countries, and also to anyone holding a valid US, UK or Schengen visa. It is a 1-year, multiple-entry visa allowing up to 90 days per stay, and it allows Umrah outside the Hajj season. Prices are quoted inconsistently, so here is the honest picture: most 2026 guides put the all-in online price at SAR 480–535 (the SAR 300 fee + about SAR 180 of insurance + VAT and processing), while Visit Saudi's own listing showed SAR 395 including insurance in October 2026. The amount on the checkout screen at visa.visitsaudi.com is the final truth on the day you apply.

How the Umrah visa is sold: The dedicated Umrah visa is issued through the Nusuk Umrah platform (umrah.nusuk.sa) or a licensed agent. Under the 2026 rule it is no longer sold standalone — it must be tied to a confirmed Nusuk-verified hotel and transport booking, and those bookings are part of the application, not an afterthought. If you are still setting the app up, our Nusuk App Guide walks through every screen, and our Umrah Visa 2026 guide explains the visa types and requirements in full.

The permit is not the visa: The Umrah permit you book in the Nusuk app once you have your visa is a separate thing — and it is free. You need both: a paid visa to enter the Kingdom, and the free permit to enter Masjid al-Haram for Umrah at your booked time. The same app books your Madinah visit — our Rawdah Permit — Step by Step guide walks through that booking — and before you pay for any visa, our Best Time to Perform Umrah guide helps you choose dates when crowds and prices are kinder.

The free stopover option: If your journey connects through Saudi Arabia on Saudia or flynas, you can take a free 96-hour stopover visa and perform Umrah during the stop. For a short visit built around a connection, that can remove the visa cost from your budget entirely.

Key dates for the 1448 AH Umrah season:

  • Visa issuance begins: 31 May 2026
  • Last Umrah visa issued: 9 March 2027
  • Last entry into the Kingdom: 23 March 2027
  • All Umrah-visa pilgrims must leave by: 7 April 2027
  • Use it or lose it: An Umrah visa must be used to enter within 30 days of issuance, or it is cancelled.

Pilgrims from Pakistan and India: You cannot apply directly through Nusuk — your Umrah visa must come through a licensed agent. On top of the official SAR 400–480, agents typically add a service fee of SAR 100–300, so ask for the government fee, the insurance and the service charge as separate lines before you pay. Agents in Pakistan currently report visa-only prices around PKR 48,000–65,000, before flights, hotels and transport are added.

Quick questions:

  • Which visa is cheapest for Umrah? The dedicated Umrah visa — about SAR 400–480 in government fee and insurance before agent charges. The tourist eVisa usually costs more (SAR 480–535 in most 2026 guides, though Visit Saudi has listed SAR 395 including insurance), but it is multiple-entry and valid for a year, so it can work out better if you plan to return. And if you are only connecting through the Kingdom on Saudia or flynas, the free 96-hour stopover visa beats both.
  • Is the Nusuk permit free? Yes. The Umrah permit booked in the Nusuk app is completely free — it is separate from your visa. Anyone charging you for the permit itself is charging for their time, not for a government fee.
  • Can I extend my stay? Plan to leave before your permitted stay ends — an Umrah visa must be entered within 30 days of issuance, and for the 1448 AH season every Umrah-visa pilgrim must be out of the Kingdom by 7 April 2027. Overstaying brings penalties, so build your return flight inside the dates and confirm the current rules on the official portal before you travel.
