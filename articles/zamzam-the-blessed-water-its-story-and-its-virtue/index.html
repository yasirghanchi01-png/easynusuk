Zamzam: The Blessed Water — Its Story and Its Virtue

Zamzam sprang up by Allah's command for Hajrah (AS) and her infant son Ismail (AS) in the empty valley of Makkah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "May Allah have mercy on the mother of Ismail. Had she left Zamzam alone, it would have been a flowing spring" (Sahih al-Bukhari 3364).

Authentic narrations: Abu Dharr (RA) lived for thirty days and nights in Makkah with no food except Zamzam; the Prophet ﷺ said of it: "It is blessed; it is food that nourishes" — and in one narration, "and a cure for sickness" (Sahih Muslim 2473). And he ﷺ said: "The water of Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for" (Sunan Ibn Majah 3062) — so drink it with a sincere intention and dua.
  • Drink facing the Qibla, saying Bismillah, in three breaths, until you are full
  • The dua taught at Zamzam: "O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant provision and healing from every illness"
  • Benefit is from Allah alone — Zamzam is a blessed means, not a substitute for medicine
